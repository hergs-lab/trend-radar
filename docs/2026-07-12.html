Platform

TikTok

TK-01
"That Was Easy" Format
Creators showcase moments where complex tasks work perfectly on the first try, capturing satisfying "wait… that's it?" energy.
Using trending Beyoncé audio, creators overlay text explaining what happened to make viewers think "there's no way it was that easy." Perfect for demonstrating how products simplify complicated processes. The format rewards speed and surprise over polish.
Doing it well
Productivity creators — Planning entire weeks in under 60 minutes with satisfying task management demos
Product demo accounts — Apps that solve frustrating workflows with one-tap solutions
Fast ROI Product-friendly Audio-driven
TK-02
Portable POV Formats
Fast, repeatable templates like "what it feels like," "how these sound to me," and "me getting kicked out" work because creators can swap in niche details without rebuilding the joke.
Format portability is the biggest pattern in July 2026. Creators are choosing mechanics that cross categories — embodied POV, absurd contrast, sensory payoff. The algorithm rewards content that can be filmed quickly while trends are still alive, with 7–10 day lifespans typical.
Doing it well
Micro-creators — Split-screen comparisons pulling 5.3M views from 11K-follower accounts
Reaction creators — Sidewalk filming getting 8.9M views from 2.9K accounts
High velocity Low barrier Niche-portable
TK-03
Event Energy Content
July rewards creators who show up early — before the premiere, before the drop, before the hot take has been taken.
Blockbuster anticipation and summer chaos drive engagement. The "Bangladesh" sound (49-second singer-songwriter track) broke TikTok's brain with escalating absurd demands. Hot Dog Summer energy (Legally Blonde 2 audio) spikes July 3-5. Cultural timing matters more than production quality.
Doing it well
Pop culture commentators — First-mover advantage on movie releases and drops
Seasonal content creators — Summer nostalgia formats that loop back annually
Time-sensitive Seasonal Fandom-driven
Platform

Instagram

IG-01
Sketch-to-Outfit Transition
Hand-drawn animation reveals where each clothing item is illustrated onto the creator's body, then seamlessly transforms into actual clothing.
One of the most visually satisfying formats on Reels right now. Creator @taliadunlop popularized it — each piece illustrated one at a time before transforming. Paired with trending orchestral audio. No business account restrictions confirmed, making it accessible for brands.
Doing it well
@taliadunlop — Format originator with smooth illustration-to-reality transitions
Fashion retailers — Product showcases that feel artistic rather than promotional
High save rate Visual storytelling Brand-safe
IG-02
"Wipe the Camera" Reveal
Empty or underwhelming space, hand swiping across lens, then instant reveal of finished look or transformation — venues are eating this up.
Format originated with @james.and.antler (Brisbane coffee bar). Original audio accessible to business accounts with no restrictions. Boney M. and R3HAB track "Sunny" anchors the transition. Works for before/after product demos, location reveals, and transformation content.
Doing it well
@james.and.antler — Brisbane venue using original audio for clean product reveals
Hospitality brands — Cafes and restaurants showcasing space transformations
Business-friendly Location-ready Product demo
IG-03
DM Shares Over Likes
Instagram's 2026 algorithm weights DM shares 3-5x higher than likes for Reels distribution — 100 likes matter less than 10 DM shares.
Adam Mosseri confirmed sends carry the most weight for reaching new audiences. When someone DMs your Reel to a friend, they're vouching for it — Instagram rewards that trust signal with broader distribution. Shareable content creates a flywheel effect for discovery.
Doing it well
Relatable meme adapters — Industry-specific meme formats outperform original content in shareability
Reaction creators — Real-time takes on news/events published within hours
Algorithmic priority Shareability Distribution shift
Platform

LinkedIn

LI-01
Document Posts (PDF Carousels)
Native document posts hit 6.6–7% engagement rate — the highest of any LinkedIn format and more than double standard text posts.
Carousels get 17x more interactions than images, yet images are still posted 6x more. LinkedIn's algorithm prioritizes dwell time — how long people engage with content, not just clicks. Document posts support educational content and storytelling, making them effective across funnel stages.
Doing it well
B2B educators — Framework breakdowns and step-by-step guides that get saved and reshared
Thought leaders — Data-rich visuals that establish authority without external links
Highest engagement Native format Save-worthy
LI-02
Clicks Are the New Invisible Metric
Between 2025 and 2026, average clicks per post rose 4.90% while overall engagement jumped to 13.90% — LinkedIn tracks every carousel swipe and "see more" click.
Likes and comments are visible, but clicks do heavy lifting for account authority. Quality and clickability now matter more than posting frequency. Posts with questions get 77% more comments. First 48 hours deliver 50% of total impressions.
Doing it well
Poll creators — Interactive polls paired with thoughtful captions drive participation
Question-led posts — Direct asks generate 77% more comments than average posts
Engagement shift Algorithm factor Interaction quality
LI-03
"Build in Public" Transparency
Behind-the-scenes content and authentic storytelling outperform polished corporate posts — audiences trust employees more than influencers or CEOs.
LinkedIn has embraced the creator economy with AI-assisted tools for content production. Video engagement jumped 34% year-over-year. Videos under 90 seconds perform best. Personal lessons, practical tactics, and real failures build authentic connection that drives long-term trust.
Doing it well
Founder-led content — Personal journey posts mixing experience with professional insights
Employee advocates — Team members sharing work process and real results
Authenticity wins Video-first Trust-building
Platform

YouTube

YT-01
Serialized Content Returns
Creators are embracing traditional TV-style formats — viewers prefer channels that offer structured arcs, recurring formats, and deeper value over one-off posts.
Druski's "Coulda Been House" reality franchise attracted 710K viewers in a year. Quenlin Blackwell's "feeding starving celebrities" series combined humor with interview format. Limited series (under 10 episodes) create binge appeal without viewer fatigue. Fans feel included in ongoing narratives.
Doing it well
Druski — Big Brother-style "Coulda Been" franchise with raw improv energy
Glitch Studio — Behind-the-scenes "Glitch Direct" format pulling fans into production
Long-form revival Bingeable Community-building
YT-02
Shorts + Long-Form Hybrid
Channels mixing Shorts for discovery with long-form for retention and monetization outperform Shorts-only channels — both formats serve different jobs.
YouTube Shorts crossed 70 billion daily views. Creators earn $0.03–$0.10 per 1,000 Shorts views vs ~$3 RPM for long-form. The real story is the funnel — Shorts act as discovery mechanism feeding long-form content and channel subscriptions. Strongest 2026 channels publish both.
Doing it well
Multi-format creators — Shorts for reach, 10+ minute videos for depth and ad revenue
Educational channels — Quick tips in Shorts, comprehensive guides in long-form
Dual strategy Monetization Discovery funnel
YT-03
Authenticity Over Polish
Creators are ditching highly-produced videos for authentic, unfiltered content — thumbnails getting simpler, fancy edits disappearing, storylines more relaxed.
Sam Sulek leads this trend. Dry Creek Wrangler School pivoted to uncut videos sharing life lessons. Viewers want texture, uncertainty, reactions — content that feels like an actual human made a choice in public. Polished uploads say "we prepared this." Authentic uploads say "we're inside the moment with you."
Doing it well
Sam Sulek — Bodybuilder at forefront of raw, unfiltered content movement
Dry Creek Wrangler School — Uncut videos with sincere life lessons, originally a horse channel
Raw content Intimacy-driven Personality-led
Always in the mix
