PLATFORM 01
TikTok
TK-01
Absurdist Audio Escalation
Pre-built emotional architecture that lets creators plug brand messages into culturally recognized frames.
Audio formats like "Bangladesh" start believable then turn ridiculous, offering instant joke structure. Creators avoid building meaning from scratch—surprise arrives fast, and platforms reward watch-through on absurd escalation hooks.
DOING IT WELL
Ian McConnell — original Bangladesh audio is fueling the format across FYP
Lifestyle brands — adapting carefully when tone aligns with brand voice
ABSURDIST FAST SURPRISE AUDIO-LED
TK-02
Netflix Documentary Sit-Down
Lightly staged conversational format that gives creators a structured entry to storytelling without scripting every word.
Creators sit in a chair, facing camera, delivering a story or observation as if being interviewed for a documentary. The format works because it offers instant sentiment structure—audiences recognize the visual language and lean in for narrative payoff.
DOING IT WELL
Shayla Crowder — tracking this as a crossover from TikTok with independent Instagram momentum
Startup founders — using it to compress origin stories into 60-second clips
NARRATIVE CONVERSATIONAL LOW-PRODUCTION
TK-03
Faceless Niche Channels (AI-Driven)
No camera, no face—just tight scripts, screen recordings, and AI voiceover flooding productivity and finance feeds.
AI lip-sync videos and AI voiceovers paired with stock B-roll are doing high volume. The algorithm still rewards these when the hook holds attention. Finance, productivity, and "did you know" content scale well because information is the draw, not the presenter.
DOING IT WELL
Finance explainer accounts — tight 30-second scripts with high completion rates
Productivity tool channels — screen recording demos with AI narration performing at scale
AI VOICEOVER FACELESS SCALABLE
PLATFORM 02
Instagram
IG-01
Single-Take Low-Production Reels
Low-production single-take content is outperforming polished posts—authenticity beats polish in August 2026.
A truck driver lip sync pulled 336 million views. YAP format (selfie, off the cuff, minimal editing, subtitles) is dominating the personal brand space. Instagram's algorithm is rewarding rawness and real over curated perfection as audiences reject over-edited feels.
DOING IT WELL
Max Tornow — entrepreneur using YAP format for business coaching content
Truck driver lip sync account — 336M views with zero production value, pure relatability
RAW SELFIE-STYLE UNPOLISHED
IG-02
Nostalgic Tribute Carousels
Olivia Rodrigo audios are driving emotional tribute carousels that lean into nostalgia and sentiment-driven engagement.
Creators use childhood photos, "me at the same age as my parents" comparisons, and memory-triggered content set to trending audio like "u + me = <3". The format works because it taps universal emotional beats and invites saves, shares, and comment storytelling.
DOING IT WELL
Lifestyle creators — using childhood photo transitions to build emotional connection
Small business owners — adapting format to show origin stories and founder journeys
NOSTALGIA EMOTIONAL CAROUSEL
IG-03
Subtitle Gratitude Format
No-licensing-required text-based Reels offering business accounts an easy win with authentic voice.
Creators overlay text directly on simple visuals, sharing gratitude moments, team shout-outs, or customer appreciation stories. The format requires no trending audio, performs well algorithmically because completion rates are high, and feels genuinely human—not corporate.
DOING IT WELL
B2B service brands — using client success stories in text overlay format
Wellness accounts — sharing daily affirmations and community gratitude posts
TEXT-BASED NO AUDIO ACCESSIBLE
PLATFORM 03
LinkedIn
LI-01
Native Carousels (PDFs)
Native document posts lead all formats with 7% engagement rate—a 14% YoY increase.
Carousels earn 1,451 impressions per post and double the engagement of video or single images. LinkedIn's algorithm tracks every swipe and "see more" click as engagement signals. The format works for educational content and storytelling across funnel stages.
DOING IT WELL
B2B SaaS marketers — breaking down frameworks into 6–8 slide carousels
Consultants — using data-rich visuals to demonstrate expertise and drive saves
HIGHEST ENGAGEMENT SWIPEABLE NATIVE
LI-02
Behind-the-Scenes Horizontal Video
Raw, unpolished 3–6 minute horizontal clips outlining thought process are driving excellent engagement in 2026.
Creators post simple, helpful frameworks in non-thought-leader-y language. The algorithm rewards plain language over corporate tone. Audiences are "starved for simple & helpful information," and BTS-style posts feel human, not performative. Completion drives reach.
DOING IT WELL
Agency founders — sharing work process and frameworks in casual desk-side format
Freelance creators — documenting client work approach with minimal editing
HORIZONTAL PROCESS-DRIVEN UNPOLISHED
LI-03
"Invisible" Interaction: Clicks Over Comments
Clicks are doing the heavy lifting for account authority—average clicks per post rose 4.9% YoY to 102.
LinkedIn tracks every carousel swipe, external link click, and "see more" expansion as engagement. Overall engagement rates jumped from 12.21% to 13.9% even as posting frequency dropped 10%. Quality and clickability now matter more than volume.
DOING IT WELL
Data-driven marketers — using interactive polls and external links to drive measurable action
SaaS product teams — posting with CTAs that encourage profile visits and link clicks
CLICKABILITY QUALITY > VOLUME ALGORITHM SIGNAL
PLATFORM 04
YouTube
YT-01
Recurring Series Formats
Viewers crave structure—recurring episodes create habitual viewing and boost distribution over time.
Druski's "Coulda Been House" and Quenlin Blackwell's "Feeding Starving Celebrities" are driving massive growth. Limited series (under 10 episodes) work best—familiar structure with creator chaos keeps viewers hooked. Glitch Studio's "Glitch Direct" BTS format builds transparency.
DOING IT WELL
Druski — reality franchises like Coulda Been House attracted 710K viewers in a year
Glitch Studio — Glitch Direct previews upcoming series in transparent vlog style
STRUCTURED ARCS RECURRING BINGE APPEAL
YT-02
Longer-Form Video (1–3 Minutes)
Mid-length video now outperforms 15-second clips on watch time, retention, and FYP distribution.
YouTube Shorts reached 200 billion daily views in 2025, but 1–3 minute structured content is where depth happens. Viewers watch short snippets to discover creators, then watch longer content to build connection. Series and multi-part explainers reward those who stay.
DOING IT WELL
Educational channels — 8–20 minute essays and guides that reward viewer commitment
Commentary creators — structured mid-length videos blending hooks with depth
MID-LENGTH DEPTH RETENTION
YT-03
Interactive & Live Formats
Live streams with structured segments and interactive tools are giving creators real-time audience participation.
Formats include news recaps, "office hours," watch parties, and co-commentary. Tools like polls, Q&A modules, and clickable overlays encourage participation. Live video is useful for testing ideas quickly—concepts that resonate can be turned into polished uploads later.
DOING IT WELL
Gaming streamers — live watch parties with audience commentary and real-time polls
Educators & coaches — hosting Q&A office hours with live demos and shoppable overlays
LIVE INTERACTIVE REAL-TIME
Always in the mix
