TikTok

Audio-driven formats and cultural moments dominating FYP

TK-01
"Self Aware" Cinematic Reflections
Temper City's "Self Aware" audio powers dreamy, introspective content that pairs aesthetic visuals with meaningful text overlays.
The format is flexible and feels calm, confident, or quietly empowering. Creators use it to show how life feels easier or more intentional, making content feel personal and memorable through the combination of beautiful visuals and thoughtful messaging.
DOING IT WELL
Productivity & wellness creators
Showing balanced routines paired with text about clarity and control
Lifestyle & dating accounts
Capturing intentional moments with overlays about being more present
Aesthetic Text Overlay 434K+ Videos
TK-02
"Reali-Tea" — Unfiltered In-Progress
TikTok's 2026 forecast names this the defining shift: audiences want honest, behind-the-scenes moments over polished perfection.
Hashtags like #lockedin, #hygiene, and #joblife signal accountability and grounded day-in-the-life content. Brands have room for process videos, creator diaries, mini-vlogs, work-in-public content, and genuine product-in-use footage that builds trust through transparency.
DOING IT WELL
Oreo (@oreo)
Reading the room in real time, mirroring audience lingo and behavior
Day-in-the-life creators
Showing unfiltered routines and accountability-focused content
Authenticity Behind-the-Scenes Platform Priority
TK-03
"Oh Ok Because" Wordplay Walk
Azealia Banks' "212" instrumental drives a confident box-step format where wordplay text overlays split compound words into casual observations.
The trend rewards creativity — the more unexpected the word split, the better the comment section reacts. It's dead simple but makes anyone look cool walking. Brands can easily play this format with product or service puns while keeping clips under 15 seconds.
DOING IT WELL
Comedic creators & brands
Using clever wordplay like "our latte was a lot" for instant relatability
Box-step dancers
Nailing the confident walk paired with perfectly-timed text reveals
Comedy Wordplay Brand-Friendly

Instagram

Transformation formats and nostalgic carousels driving saves

IG-01
"World Stop!" Transformation
Creator @browsbyzulema's breakout format is a one-take before-and-after commanding attention through audio mechanics and dramatic pause.
This transformation trend works across beauty, fashion, fitness, and home content. The format drives high completion rates and works because it delivers instant gratification. Instagram's originality scoring rewards fresh filming, so reposts from other platforms won't perform.
DOING IT WELL
@browsbyzulema
Originated the format with perfectly-timed audio and visual transformation
Beauty & fitness creators
Adapting one-take reveals to showcase makeup, workouts, and styling
Breakout Format Before/After High Saves
IG-02
Harry Styles "Aperture" Moments
Aperture has become the go-to sound for reflective, cinematic Reels that feel polished without trying too hard.
The audio has a natural build perfect for storytelling, recaps, and visually satisfying moments. This trend leans into mood over punchlines — soft transitions, meaningful clips, and moments worth pausing on. It works equally well for personal storytelling and clean brand visuals.
DOING IT WELL
Lifestyle & GRWM creators
Pairing aesthetic morning routines with the track's cinematic build
Brand storytellers
Using soft transitions to showcase product journeys and spring content
Trending Audio Cinematic Mood-Driven
IG-03
Native Affiliate Tagging
Instagram's new feature lets creators tag up to 30 affiliate products directly in Reels, with conversion data flowing inside Meta.
This shifts where creator commerce data lives, changing the leverage dynamic between brands and third-party platforms. Brands can now see which Reels drove clicks and purchases directly in Meta, without routing through LTK or ShopMy. The feature scales through spring 2026 as a conversion tracking advantage.
DOING IT WELL
Product review creators
Tagging every item in "get ready with me" Reels for direct purchases
Brands with verified catalogs
Enabling creator partners to tag products with accurate pricing in-app
Platform Feature Commerce ROI-Driven

LinkedIn

Native documents and unpolished process videos driving engagement

LI-01
Native Document Dominance
Carousels and PDFs are the #1 performing LinkedIn format in 2026 with a 7% engagement rate, up 14% year-over-year.
Native documents increase "dwell time" as users swipe through, which the algorithm rewards. Multi-page PDFs uploaded as single documents outperform image galleries. The format is ideal for repurposing blog posts, event photos, and data visualizations into swipeable storytelling.
DOING IT WELL
B2B marketers & agencies
Turning case studies and reports into carousel slideshows with 100+ clicks
Thought leaders
Designing swipe-through frameworks and timelines in Canva for LinkedIn
Top Format 7% Engagement Dwell Time
LI-02
Raw Behind-the-Scenes Process
Unpolished, horizontal 3–6 minute clips outlining thought processes and frameworks are driving excellent engagement on LinkedIn in 2026.
Audiences are starved for simple, helpful information delivered in non-thought-leadery posts. Raw content that feels like real behind-the-scenes builds trust faster than polished productions. Video views dropped 36% overall, but lighthearted content and repurposed live session clips outperform everything else.
DOING IT WELL
B2B founders & consultants
Self-recording process breakdowns without heavy editing or scripts
Social teams at Socialinsider
Repurposing funny moments and live clips for high engagement
Authenticity Process Video 3-6 Min
LI-03
Engagement Farming That Works
Posts using comment-to-access hooks like "Comment 'Ads' for my template" are capitalizing on LinkedIn's 24% increase in comments and 28% jump in clicks.
Interactive posts and polls are proving rewarding as the platform's engagement rises. Thought leadership sharing expertise signals authority to the algorithm, which boosts content. Posting 2–5 times per week drives up to 5x more profile views, with twice-weekly posting being the optimal frequency.
DOING IT WELL
Marketing & SaaS creators
Using template giveaways to drive DM conversations and profile views
Personal brand builders
Posting polls with thoughtful captions to gather insights and spark interaction
Interactive Comment-Driven 24% Engagement ↑

YouTube

Serialized formats and Shorts coexisting as discovery drivers

YT-01
TV-Style Serialized Content
Creators are embracing traditional, episodic video series that give viewers recurring formats and storylines to follow across channels.
YouTube's Culture & Trends Report shows creators building "digital franchises" around multi-part explainers, reaction shows, and lore breakdowns. With more viewers watching on TV, episodic documentary-style content appeals to those seeking high-production value and immersive storytelling experiences.
DOING IT WELL
Druski (Coulda Been House)
Big Brother-style reality formats reimagined for YouTube in 10-episode arcs
Brazilian creator houses
Building sprawling shared universes requiring multi-channel viewing
Episodic TV Viewing Binge Appeal
YT-02
YouTube Shorts as Discovery
Shorts receive over 90 billion views per day and serve as the first point of contact, especially for mobile-first younger demographics.
Short-form vertical video under 90 seconds drives viewer engagement and ROI. The format is ideal for translating complex issues into digestible moments. Creators use Shorts to funnel audiences to long-form content, creating a dual-track strategy where ultra-short hooks (15–45 seconds) lead to structured mid-length videos (8–20 minutes).
DOING IT WELL
Madeline Argy
Transitioning from short-form to long-form by using Shorts as discovery
Educational creators
Using vertical cutdowns to drive traffic to full tutorials and deep dives
90B Views/Day Mobile-First Funnel Strategy
YT-03
Raw Authenticity Over Production
Creators are ditching highly-produced videos for authentic, unfiltered content with simpler thumbnails and relaxed pacing to create intimacy.
Over-produced videos now underperform as viewers see them as ads. Bodybuilder Sam Sulek and Dry Creek Wrangler School exemplify this shift toward sincere, meandering videos. Audiences crave real stories and human connection, making raw self-recorded content outperform polished brand spots.
DOING IT WELL
Sam Sulek
Uncut gym footage with relaxed pacing building parasocial intimacy
Dry Creek Wrangler School
Pivoting to uncut life lessons with genuine conversation over polish
Authenticity Low Production Intimacy-Driven
