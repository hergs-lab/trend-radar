Trending Now
TikTok
TK-01
Format-First Comedy
Short, repeatable premises with hard beat-drops — not polished lip-syncs.
The algorithm rewards short comedy formats built on structure and punchline timing rather than production value. Creators are looping the same format with fresh examples, keeping retention high and re-watch loops active.
Doing It Well
Vexbolts
4.9M followers using repeatable gaming-meme formats with tight punchline structure
Delayed-realization format users
2.4M+ likes on hindsight comedy — embarrassing memories with perfect audio timing
Algorithm boost Comedy Rewatch loops
TK-02
Realism Over Fantasy
Audiences moving away from polished curation toward unfiltered behind-the-scenes moments.
TikTok's 2026 Next report confirms the platform shift toward realism and "Emotional ROI" — users verify purchase intent through raw creator content. Brands showing real process and people outperform curated perfection across all verticals.
Doing It Well
Shayla Crowder
500K followers tracking trends from inside the feed, not dashboards
Process-first brands
Behind-the-scenes formats showing mistakes and method, not just outcomes
Platform shift Authenticity BTS content
TK-03
Three-Slide Confidence Format
Simple visual progression — two solo confidence shots, one reveal with partner or friend.
Jessie Murph's "I Like How I Look" audio is driving a clean three-panel format climbing the trending charts in September. The format works because it's visually simple, emotionally clear, and easy to adapt across niches from fashion to fitness.
Doing It Well
Fashion & lifestyle creators
Using the format for outfit transitions with emotional storytelling beats
Relationship content accounts
Two solo shots building anticipation, final reveal creating the punchline
Trending audio Visual format Easy adapt
Trending Now
Instagram
IG-01
DM Shares > Likes
Shares carry 3–5x more distribution weight than likes in the algorithm.
Adam Mosseri confirmed that DM shares are the top-weighted ranking signal for Reels. When someone sends your content to a friend, Instagram treats it as a trust signal and rewards it with broader reach. 100 likes matter less than 10 shares.
Doing It Well
Relatable format creators
Content designed to prompt "tag a friend" or "send this to someone who needs it"
Save-worthy educational accounts
Carousels and tutorials people want to revisit and share with their network
Algorithm priority Shareability Trust signal
IG-02
Potential-Maxxing with Proof
Creators backing up self-improvement claims with hard numbers instead of vague affirmations.
The format rejects motivational fluff in favor of concrete metrics — post counts, follower growth, view totals, revenue numbers. It works because it shifts the narrative from aspiration to evidence, making growth stories credible and replicable.
Doing It Well
Creator economy accounts
Showing exact follower graphs, engagement data, and revenue as proof points
Max Tornow
Business coach using data-backed growth stories in minimal-edit YAP format
Data-driven Credibility Original audio
IG-03
Yellow Font Vulnerability
Yellow text signals emotionally vulnerable or deeply honest content — or parodies it.
Yellow Font Theory positions bold yellow captions as shorthand for "this is my real thought." Creators use it for sincere confessions or absurd parodies, and both versions drive engagement because the format itself has become a trust cue.
Doing It Well
Personal brand creators
Using yellow text for life lessons and honest reflections that feel unfiltered
Comedy accounts
Parodying the format with intentionally overdramatic "confessions" for comedic effect
Visual cue Authenticity Dual-use format
Trending Now
LinkedIn
LI-01
Carousels Dominate Reach
Carousels earn 1,451 impressions per post — more than double video's 606.
Multi-image carousels outperform video and single images on both engagement rate and reach. The format works because LinkedIn tracks every swipe and "see more" click as engagement signals, boosting the post's authority in the feed.
Doing It Well
B2B thought leaders
Using carousels to share frameworks, case studies, and industry breakdowns
Employee advocacy accounts
Carousels showing process, lessons learned, and behind-the-scenes insights
Highest reach Engagement driver Click tracking
LI-02
Comment Quality Over Volume
Posts with 5+ word comments get 3x more distribution than single-word reactions.
LinkedIn's September 2026 algorithm update prioritizes substantive comment threads over vanity metrics. The platform now interprets detailed responses as signals of meaningful conversation, rewarding posts that spark discussion rather than simple emoji reactions.
Doing It Well
Posts with open-ended questions
Prompting detailed replies and discussion threads instead of one-tap reactions
Expert-led content creators
Sharing frameworks and insights that invite professional debate and shared experiences
Algorithm update Conversation depth Sept 2026
LI-03
Raw Behind-the-Scenes Video
Unpolished horizontal clips outlining thought process and frameworks are driving engagement.
Video on LinkedIn is growing (27% increase in creation, 36% in views), but the winners are 3–6 minute unpolished formats that feel like internal team briefings. People are seeking simple, helpful information without the "thought leader" veneer.
Doing It Well
Founders and operators
Sharing real decision-making frameworks and work-in-progress thinking
Sales and marketing leaders
Horizontal video walkthroughs showing tools, workflows, and honest mistakes
Video growth Unpolished Process content
Trending Now
YouTube
YT-01
Recurring Formats Build Trust
Serialized content outperforms one-offs — episodes build anticipation and return viewers.
Creators are structuring channels around recurring formats (Druski's "Coulda Been" franchise, Quenlin Blackwell's "Feeding Starving Celebrities"). Viewers want structure and predictability, treating YouTube like appointment viewing rather than random discovery.
Doing It Well
Glitch Studio
Behind-the-scenes "Glitch Direct" format previewing animation series in casual vlog style
IShowSpeed
11–17M subscriber growth from serialized "Speed Goes Pro" and meme-driven recurring bits
Series formats Return viewers Appointment viewing
YT-02
Shorts as Discovery Engine
200 billion daily Shorts views — use short-form to spark interest, long-form to convert.
YouTube CEO Neal Mohan confirmed Shorts reached 200B daily views in 2025. The winning strategy is using Shorts for discovery (mini vlogs, food, DIY, explainers) then funneling viewers to long-form demos, tutorials, or livestreams for deeper engagement and conversion.
Doing It Well
Product demo channels
Shorts teasing features, long videos showing full walkthroughs and buyer questions
Educational creators
Short-form hooks on trending questions, long-form deep-dives building authority
Funnel strategy Discovery to depth 200B views/day
YT-03
Challenge & Experiment Formats
Viewers stay for the outcome — "Did it work?" creates retention and watch time.
The experiment structure (hypothesis → process → result) drives retention because it creates a specific viewer question. "I lived for a week spending $10/day" outperforms "5 ways to save money" because the outcome is uncertain and the format is emotionally engaging.
Doing It Well
Niche-adapted challenge creators
Taking popular challenge formats and adapting them to specific verticals
Lifestyle experimenters
Creating real stakes around everyday topics — budget, productivity, fitness
High retention Outcome-driven Adaptable format
