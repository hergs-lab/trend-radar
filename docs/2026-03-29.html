Platform

TikTok

TK-01
Behind-the-Scenes Unfiltered
People are craving grounding through honesty, community, and real process over curated perfection.
TikTok's 2026 forecast shows audiences shifting from escapism to clarity. Brands showing real process and people — not polished highlights — are seeing the strongest resonance. This aligns with the "lock-in" mindset where audiences want transparency and authenticity.
DOING IT WELL
Oreo
Turned account into playful clubhouse reading the room in real time, mirroring audience lingo.
Chamberlain Coffee
Handheld, one-take videos feel like casual check-ins from a friend, unfiltered and approachable.
Authenticity BTS content Lo-fi aesthetic
TK-02
POV Relatable Moments
POV format continues dominating as powerful tool for storytelling, humor, and showing customer experiences.
The POV format came in hot in 2025 and shows no signs of slowing. Creators use it for relatable moments, product demos, and behind-the-scenes perspectives. Works because moments are simple, relatable, and the ask is usually small but the response feels like a win.
DOING IT WELL
Ryanair
Uses satirical POV style like "POV: The toilets after we add..." for comedic brand moments.
Beauty & lifestyle creators
Highlighting relatable customer experiences and everyday scenarios that resonate instantly.
Still growing Storytelling Humor
TK-03
Companion-Style Videos
Audiences want company through screens — "get ready with me," "study with me" formats pulling huge engagement.
"Study with me," "get ready with me," "eat dinner with me," and "work a 9–5 with me" formats are everywhere. People want company even through screens, creating parasocial connection. This trend reflects post-pandemic desire for ambient human presence.
DOING IT WELL
Lifestyle vloggers
Creating cozy, authentic "with me" content that makes viewers feel like they're hanging out.
Study & productivity creators
Offering accountability and companionship through long-form "study with me" sessions.
Parasocial Lo-fi Ambient presence
Platform

Instagram

IG-01
Edutainment Reels
Short-form content blending information, humor, and storytelling is becoming standard — bite-sized value wins.
The breakout theme for 2026 is "edutainment" — content that educates while entertaining. Finance experts sharing budgeting tips with clever memes, or quick punchlines spark high resonance. Instagram prioritizes Reels that keep users watching, and this format delivers completion rate + shares.
DOING IT WELL
Finance & career educators
Blending data-driven tips with humor and meme culture for shareability.
Beauty & skincare brands
Quick tutorials with entertaining hooks that teach while keeping viewers engaged.
High ROI Educational Entertaining
IG-02
Text Line Drop Format
Each jump or stomp reveals a new line of text — turns simple storytelling into addictive watching.
Trending audio "Text Line Drop" has creators film themselves centered, then with every jump/step/stomp, shift the frame to reveal hidden text. Motion keeps viewers engaged longer than static posts. Works for opinions, mini-stories, punchy explanations with final payoff.
DOING IT WELL
Storytelling creators
Using kinetic text reveals to build narrative tension and keep viewers watching.
Opinion & commentary accounts
Layering hot takes with dynamic motion to boost watch time and completion rate.
Trending audio Watch time Dynamic
IG-03
Sliding Scale Trend
Perfect for creators and brands to highlight strengths, specialties, and personality in a fun visual format.
Use text and emojis to create personal "sliding scales" that inform audiences what type of creator or professional you are. "Loud and colorful vs. minimal and classic?" This trend teaches people about your brand while being highly engaging and shareable.
DOING IT WELL
Design & branding studios
Showcasing brand aesthetic and style preferences with visual clarity and personality.
Service-based businesses
Helping audiences quickly understand their approach, tone, and unique positioning.
Brand personality Relatable Shareable
Platform

LinkedIn

LI-01
Native Document Carousels
Native documents claim top spot with 7.00% engagement rate — 14% YoY increase in performance.
LinkedIn carousels (PDF uploads) are algorithmic gold because they increase dwell time as users swipe through. They force 30-60 seconds of engagement, which is massive. Repurpose blog posts, reports, or create swipe-through storytelling with tension and reveals.
DOING IT WELL
B2B marketers & agencies
Turning case studies and data into visual carousels that drive saves and shares.
Thought leaders
Using carousel format for frameworks, step-by-step guides, and industry insights.
Top format Dwell time Educational
LI-02
Raw BTS Process Videos
Unpolished 3-6 minute horizontal clips showing thought process or frameworks are driving excellent engagement.
Raw, behind-the-scenes content showing how you approach work is resonating. People are starved for simple, helpful information in non-"thought leadery" posts. Native videos under 90 seconds with captions perform best, but longer BTS process content (3-6 min) is also thriving.
DOING IT WELL
Founders & operators
Sharing unfiltered decision-making process and frameworks in horizontal talking-head videos.
B2B service providers
Documenting real client work and methodology without over-production.
Authenticity Video growth Helpful
LI-03
Comment-Bait Lead Magnets
Posts offering guides/templates in exchange for keyword comments spark engagement spikes and push to more feeds.
The lead magnet approach proved itself in 2025 and continues in 2026. Offer a guide/template/resource, ask for keyword comment to receive it. Algorithm sees engagement spike and pushes post to more feeds. Meanwhile, you expand network with interested prospects.
DOING IT WELL
Marketing & sales consultants
Offering templates and frameworks that generate hundreds of comments and qualified leads.
SaaS & tool creators
Using valuable resources as engagement bait to build pipeline and network.
Lead gen Engagement farming Growth
Platform

YouTube

YT-01
TV-Style Serialized Content
Creators embracing traditional TV formats — serialized videos, recurring formats, episodic structure keeping viewers hooked.
YouTube's Culture & Trends Report shows nostalgic TV-style content is back. Think recurring formats with clear structure (same energy, same style, only topic changes). From cooking shows to reality-style series, audiences want binge-worthy episodic content with emotional investment.
DOING IT WELL
First We Feast (Hot Ones)
Celebrities eating spicy wings in structured interview format — hour-long episodes, recurring structure.
Druski (Coulda Been House)
Big Brother-style format reimagined for YouTube with limited series under 10 episodes.
CTV growth Episodic Bingeable
YT-02
Shorts as Long-Form Funnel
Shorts crossed 70B daily views — creators using them as discovery tool feeding long-form content and subscriptions.
Shorts act as top-of-funnel acquisition, not standalone revenue. YouTube's unique advantage: short-form directly feeds long-form monetization ecosystem. Creators seeing faster channel growth by teasing/clipping longer videos into Shorts, driving viewers to full content.
DOING IT WELL
Educational & tutorial creators
Clipping highlights from 10-min videos into Shorts, driving traffic to full tutorials.
Commentary channels
Using Shorts for hot takes that tease longer-form analysis and deep dives.
Discovery Funnel strategy Multi-format
YT-03
Sincere, Uncut Long-Form
Creators shifting toward sincere, uncut videos — raw authenticity thriving over high-production "click wars."
Sam Sulek and Dry Creek Wrangler School lead trend of posting uncut videos sharing life lessons. Simplified thumbnails and titles appear more genuine. While thumbnail testing tools exist, audiences reward creators who feel real over those chasing clicks.
DOING IT WELL
Sam Sulek
Bodybuilder posting uncut, sincere content with raw authenticity over polished editing.
Dry Creek Wrangler School
Pivoted from horse channel to uncut videos sharing life lessons with genuine vulnerability.
Authenticity Long-form Uncut
