TikTok
What's breaking through
TK-01
Faceless AI Content Explosion
AI-generated characters with tight scripts are eating the For You Page without a single face on camera.
AI lip-sync videos and AI voiceovers paired with screen recordings dominate productivity, finance, and "did you know" niches. The algorithm still rewards them when the hook holds attention. Production scales without talent availability.
DOING IT WELL
Finance & productivity channels
Faceless formats work best in info-heavy niches where content is the draw, not the presenter
Tutorial creators using screen recordings
AI voiceover + screen capture delivers 30-90 second tutorials with strong completion rates
AI-POWERED SCALABLE FACELESS
TK-02
Nostalgic Glow-Up Transitions
Creators are landing emotional beats with childhood photos timed to viral audio drops.
"Me at the Same Age as My Parents" and childhood-photo transitions are huge this month. The Paparazzi dubstep ugly-to-hot format continues to perform. These tap into both summer nostalgia and back-to-school sentimentality.
DOING IT WELL
Parent creators documenting drop-offs
Bittersweet back-to-school content hits both moods of August's dual-season vibe
Lifestyle creators using Paparazzi audio
Simple format — just dance, add bold text overlay, let the attitude carry the video
NOSTALGIC EMOTIONAL HOOK SEASONAL
TK-03
Episodic Series Formats
Recurring episodes create habitual viewing, which boosts distribution over time as a content moat.
One-off posts are losing to series content because recurring formats improve returning viewers and session time. Brands posting fewer than six times per week see 93% higher engagement than those posting at record volumes. Quality and format consistency matter more than frequency.
DOING IT WELL
Niche channels with recurring formats
Series-based content turns casual viewers into returning audiences that algorithms reward
Educational creators with episode arcs
Structured series (1-3 minutes each) outperform 15-second clips on watch time and FYP distribution
RETENTION DRIVER SERIES HABITUAL
Instagram
Reels & carousel momentum
IG-01
Low-Production Single-Take Wins
A single-take truck driver lip sync pulled 336 million views — proof that raw beats polished right now.
Instagram is rewarding content that looks personally motivated even when it's commercial. The winning Reel doesn't begin with a product pitch — it begins with a thought the viewer has had, then lets the product arrive as the resolution. Absurdist audio bits and low-fi skits are outperforming high-polish content.
DOING IT WELL
Everyday creators using relatable audio
Low-production, single-take formats feel authentic and align with Instagram's 2026 content preference
Brands starting with viewer pain points
Leading with a problem the audience recognizes, not a product pitch, creates resonant openings
AUTHENTIC RAW RELATABLE
IG-02
"And for the Lady..." Personalization Trend
Creators reveal the perfect product or experience tailored to a specific type of person — an easy showcase format for brands.
Using Noah Kahan's "End of August" as the soundtrack, creators introduce something that perfectly matches a personality, lifestyle, or mood. The format adapts well beyond "lady" to any niche: creator, entrepreneur, gym rat, student, traveler. It's a clean, relatable way to demonstrate audience fit.
DOING IT WELL
Service brands targeting specific personas
Easy format to showcase exactly who your offering is made for with clear audience segmentation
Lifestyle influencers with niche communities
Adapting the phrase to fit their audience (e.g., "for the small business owner") drives relevance
PERSONALIZATION BRAND-FRIENDLY TRENDING AUDIO
IG-03
Netflix Documentary Chair-Sit Format
This crossed over from TikTok and is already trending independently on Instagram — rare confirmation of real staying power.
Set a slow, swelling documentary score, place a chair in a meaningful location, and let the visual storytelling do the work. The format works because it's simple, visually distinct, and instantly recognizable. Under 30 seconds is the sweet spot for completion.
DOING IT WELL
Storytelling creators with cinematic sensibility
The format rewards visual composition and mood-setting over dialogue or explanation
Location-based accounts (travel, real estate)
A meaningful setting paired with documentary audio creates instant narrative intrigue
CROSS-PLATFORM VISUAL STORYTELLING CINEMATIC
LinkedIn
Algorithm shifts & engagement patterns
LI-01
Native Documents Dominate
PDF carousels hit 6.6% engagement — the highest of any LinkedIn format and more than double standard video.
Document posts (carousels/PDFs) lead all formats with a 7% engagement rate, up 14% year-over-year. They align with how users consume content on LinkedIn: educational, swipeable, and saveable. Both educational content and storytelling work in this format across different funnel stages.
DOING IT WELL
B2B thought leaders sharing frameworks
Carousels with clear frameworks or step-by-step breakdowns earn saves and extended engagement
Agencies packaging case studies as PDFs
Native documents support both education and storytelling, working across the entire funnel
HIGHEST ENGAGEMENT CAROUSEL SAVEABLE
LI-02
Invisible Interactions Matter More
Clicks are doing the heavy lifting for account authority — LinkedIn tracks every carousel swipe and "see more" link.
Between 2025 and 2026, average clicks per post rose from 97.54 to 102.32 (up 4.90%), even as posting frequency dropped nearly 10%. Quality and "clickability" now matter more than posting volume. Carousels, polls, and posts with external links are more likely to stop the scroll.
DOING IT WELL
Creators using multi-slide carousels
Every swipe is tracked as an engagement signal, rewarding formats that encourage interaction
Thought leaders posting less but higher quality
Fewer, better posts with strong hooks earn more clicks than high-volume posting strategies
CLICK-DRIVEN QUALITY > VOLUME ALGORITHM SHIFT
LI-03
Raw Behind-the-Scenes Video
Unpolished 3-6 minute horizontal clips outlining thought processes are showing excellent engagement.
People are starved for simple, helpful information delivered cleanly without "thought-leadery" polish. Raw, behind-the-scenes-feeling posts that outline frameworks or processes are contributing to higher engagement. LinkedIn rewards plain language in 2026 more than at any previous point.
DOING IT WELL
Founders sharing real work processes
3-6 minute horizontal videos showing actual workflows feel authentic and provide real value
Consultants breaking down frameworks simply
Plain language and unpolished delivery outperform corporate tone and overproduced content
UNPOLISHED HELPFUL PLAIN LANGUAGE
YouTube
Long-form, Shorts, and series tactics
YT-01
Structured Series Over One-Offs
Viewers prefer channels with structured arcs and recurring formats that deliver deeper value over time.
IShowSpeed's "Speed Goes Pro" series and Druski's "Coulda Been House" Big Brother-style format both drove massive growth. Limited series (under 10 episodes) create binge appeal without viewer fatigue. Series formats improve returning viewers and session time, which the algorithm rewards.
DOING IT WELL
IShowSpeed (@IShowSpeed)
Speed Goes Pro turned sports chaos into a structured recurring show, gaining 11-17M subs in a year
Druski (Coulda Been House creator)
Big Brother-style format with creator chaos — familiar structure flipped with raw improv energy
SERIES FORMAT RECURRING BINGE APPEAL
YT-02
Ambient Long-Session Content
Hour-plus videos target "study with me" and sleep playlists where watch time per view is enormous.
Ghibli-styled animated cooking videos with lofi piano are scoring 10-minute to 1-hour+ runtimes. Two proven genres stacked: Ghibli visuals have huge search demand, cooking ASMR has enormous session length. Together they compound. The deep back catalogue keeps earning from suggested long after upload.
DOING IT WELL
Ghibli cooking animation channels
Clay pot fish in Vietnam, chicken adobo during typhoons — cuisine as a content engine with global appeal
ASMR and ambient lifestyle creators
Long-session formats where every country/recipe is a new video using the same repeatable pipeline
LONG-FORM HIGH WATCH TIME AMBIENT
YT-03
Shorts as Repeatable Formats
Mini vlogs, food, dance, DIY, and explainers dominate because they're easy to post consistently without burning out.
Shorts reached 200 billion average daily views in 2025. Trending formats cluster around fast comprehension, strong retention, rewatch loops, and instantly recognizable structures. The fastest path to trending is choosing formats you can repeat weekly without losing quality.
DOING IT WELL
Tutorial creators with repeatable templates
DIY and explainer Shorts work because the format is infinitely repeatable across different topics
Food and lifestyle vloggers
Mini vlogs and food content have fast comprehension and high rewatch loops, perfect for Shorts
