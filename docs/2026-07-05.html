TikTok
Event energy dominates the FYP
TK-01
Behind-the-Scenes "Reali-Tea"
Audiences are moving away from curated aesthetics toward honesty, real process, and community participation.
TikTok's 2026 trend report confirms users want unfiltered stories and BTS moments over perfection. Brands showing real people and real processes are outperforming polished content. The shift from "delulu" escapism to grounded realism is reshaping the FYP.
DOING IT WELL
Duracell — Traced search journeys to find K-pop fans powering light sticks, turned niche discovery into growth strategy
Educational how-to channels — 16.1% of best-performing brand posts are educational content driving saves and rewatches
Authenticity BTS Content Process Over Polish
TK-02
UGC-Style Creator-Led Video
Lo-fi, first-person content that looks like it came from a real user outperforms polished production by +55% ROI.
Content with minimal logos or overlays beats traditional ad formats by +81% ROI. The algorithm rewards native-feeling content that doesn't trigger "scroll past" behaviour. Unbranded UGC performs +19% better than branded versions.
DOING IT WELL
E-commerce brands (Precis analysis) — UGC outperformed non-UGC by +55% ROI across 10 brands studied
Small brands with creator partnerships — Using creators' authentic voice instead of brand scripts for native feed integration
UGC Format Native Content Creator Voice
TK-03
Specific, Descriptive Hashtags
Hashtag traffic grew 114% year-over-year; posts with 1-5 specific hashtags get 5% more views and 10% more interactions.
Generic tags like #fyp are dead. The algorithm now uses specific hashtags to place content correctly. A pasta recipe performs better with #pastarecipes or #weeknightdinner than broad tags. The more specific the hashtag, the better the audience match.
DOING IT WELL
Food creators — Using micro-niche tags like #15minutemeals instead of #cooking
Fitness accounts — Specific tags like #homegluteworkout outperform generic #fitness
Hashtag Revival Algorithmic Placement Niche Tags
Instagram
DM shares now weighted 3–5× higher than likes
IG-01
DM Share–Optimised Reels
When someone sends your Reel to a friend, Instagram interprets it as a trust signal and rewards it with broader distribution.
Buffer's April 2026 analysis shows DM shares are weighted 3–5× higher than likes for reach. 100 likes matter less than 10 shares. The shift from vanity metrics to trust signals is reshaping content strategy — creators optimize for "send to a friend" moments.
DOING IT WELL
Relatable meme accounts — Creating "tag a friend" and "send this to someone who..." hooks
Educational creators — Shareable tips formatted as saveable mini-guides
DM Shares Trust Signal Algorithm Weight
IG-02
Raw "YAP Format" Selfie Videos
Minimally edited, talking-head style content counters over-edited feel and AI-generic content by feeling genuinely human.
Entrepreneur Max Tornow's YAP videos (selfie videos, off the cuff, no script, minimal editing with subtitles) have amassed 800K+ views. Manychat's 2026 report shows audiences want less polish. The format works because it signals real human presence in an AI-saturated feed.
DOING IT WELL
@maxtornow — Business coach using unscripted selfie videos hitting 800K+ views
Personal brand builders — Ditching cinematic production for authentic, single-take talking head content
Anti-Polish Selfie Format Authenticity
IG-03
Carousel Engagement Surge
Carousels hit 0.72% engagement rate, up 30.9% from 2025, outperforming single images and video for saves and repeat visits.
Carousels force users to spend 30–60 seconds with content. They win on saves, education, and repeat visibility. Reels bring new attention; carousels structure insight and step-by-step content that buyers revisit later. Both formats serve different jobs in the funnel.
DOING IT WELL
Educational brands — Using carousels for frameworks, checklists, step-by-step guides
Design & marketing agencies — Multi-slide case studies and before/after transformations
Carousels Saves Educational
LinkedIn
Saves have been the most important factor for ages
LI-01
Native Documents (Carousels/PDFs)
Document posts lead all formats with 7% engagement rate — 17× more interactions than images, yet posted 6× less often.
Metricool's 2026 study shows carousels earn 1,451 average impressions vs. 605 for video. Multi-image posts hit 3.71% engagement vs. 1.80% for video. The disconnect: 75% of content is images/video, but native documents outperform both. Carousels support educational content and storytelling across funnel stages.
DOING IT WELL
B2B SaaS brands — Using carousel case studies and step-by-step frameworks
Thought leaders — Repurposing blog posts into 8–10 slide native documents
Carousels Native Format High Engagement
LI-02
Saveable, Referenceable Content
Saves carry more weight than quick likes; posts people bookmark resurface in feeds for weeks, compounding reach over time.
LinkedIn expert Richard Donnelly confirms "saves have been the most important factor for ages." Create frameworks, checklists, and practical guides that people want to reference later. If your post contains bookmarkable information, the algorithm distributes it. Posts including a direct question get 77% more comments.
DOING IT WELL
Marketing strategists — Posting step-by-step content calendars and planning templates
Consultants — Sharing tactical frameworks users revisit during implementation
Saves Utility Long-Term Value
LI-03
Short Native Video Under 90 Seconds
Native videos with captions and a strong hook in the first 3 seconds deliver ~5.5% engagement rates — highest among all content types.
Hootsuite's data confirms video achieves the highest engagement on LinkedIn. The platform is pushing video harder to compete with TikTok-style consumption. LinkedIn deployed AI to scan posts for original ideas and real expertise — generic or AI content gets demoted. Video engagement jumped 34% compared to last year.
DOING IT WELL
Founders and CEOs — 60-second talking-head videos sharing lessons learned
Recruiters — Quick tips on resume mistakes with clear CTAs
Video Native Upload Hook First
YouTube
Recurring formats and structured arcs win over one-offs
YT-01
Serialized, TV-Style Reality Formats
Viewers prefer structured arcs, recurring formats, and episodic series over one-off uploads — building anticipation and binge appeal.
Druski's "Coulda Been House" reality franchise attracted 710K subscribers. IShowSpeed's "Speed Goes Pro" turned sports chaos into a structured show. Quenlin Blackwell's "feeding starving celebrities" became a humor-driven interview series. Limited series (under 10 episodes) avoid viewer fatigue while driving repeat viewership.
DOING IT WELL
@druski — Reality franchises like "Coulda Been House" with Big Brother-style format
@IShowSpeed — "Speed Goes Pro" series turning sports moments into structured episodes
Serialized Recurring Format Episodic
YT-02
Long-Form + Shorts Hybrid Strategy
Channels mixing Shorts for discovery with long-form for retention and monetization outperform Shorts-only channels in 2026.
After years of Shorts dominance, watch time on long-form (10+ minutes) is growing again. Shorts drive discovery and reach; long-form drives watch time, ad revenue, and depth. YouTube Shorts crossed 70B daily views and creators can now earn money ($0.03–$0.10 per 1K views). The real story: Shorts act as a funnel to long-form subscriptions.
DOING IT WELL
Madeline Argy — Jumped from short-form to long-form, balancing both formats
Edutainment channels — Using Shorts for hooks, long-form for deep dives
Hybrid Strategy Shorts Funnel Long-Form ROI
YT-03
Authentic, Unfiltered Creator Presence
Creators are ditching highly-produced videos for authentic, unfiltered content with simpler thumbnails and relaxed pacing to create intimacy.
Bodybuilder Sam Sulek leads the trend. Dry Creek Wrangler School pivoted to uncut life lesson videos. Thumbnails are getting simpler and fancy edits are disappearing. The shift: from cinematic production to sincere, meandering storytelling. Audiences value genuine connection over polish. Even storylines now have relaxed pacing.
DOING IT WELL
@SamSulek — Unedited gym vlogs with minimal production, raw personality
Dry Creek Wrangler School — Uncut videos sharing life lessons in authentic, unscripted tone
Authenticity Unfiltered Intimacy Over Production
