TikTok
What's Moving Now
TK-01
AI-Generated Fantasy Content
Weird, funny AI-generated clips are going viral at high speed, giving creators who move fast an edge over polished content.
The algorithm rewards quick adaptation. AI tools let even small creators produce "professional-looking" content that feels energetic enough to hold attention through the entire clip. It's not about replacing creativity—it's another editing tool in the workflow.
DOING IT WELL
Pippit AI creators
Using AI workflow tools to package strong hooks and turn briefs into social-ready output fast.
Fantasy/comedy micro-creators
Posting AI fantasy clips with weird humor that gets shared rapidly in niche communities.
Speed wins AI tools High velocity
TK-02
Glitch Transition Edits
July's dominant editing style uses glitch transitions to keep viewers watching through the entire clip—a key algorithm factor.
The result feels energetic and prevents drop-off. Editing styles evolve monthly, but glitch transitions are holding because they create visual momentum that signals "something's about to happen," keeping retention high through completion.
DOING IT WELL
Fashion transition creators
Using glitch cuts in outfit reveals to create anticipation and boost rewatch loops.
Transformation video accounts
Pairing glitch effects with before/after content to maximize completion rates.
Retention hack Editing trend July format
TK-03
Authenticity Over Polish
TikTok's 2026 forecast highlights the shift from fantasy to realism—honesty and process-focused storytelling drive engagement this year.
The platform is moving away from highly polished influencer content. Audiences prefer "Emotional ROI" where they verify purchases through TikTok before buying. Real stories, real reactions, and visible stakes perform better than overproduced content.
DOING IT WELL
Process storytellers
Sharing unfiltered behind-the-scenes and thought processes that feel human and timely.
Niche community accounts
Deep-diving into relatable topics with AR filters and authentic skits that connect emotionally.
Verified by TikTok Raw content Emotional ROI
Instagram
Reels & Formats Winning
IG-01
Netflix Documentary Chair-Sit
A TikTok crossover that's independently trending on Instagram—proof the format has real staying power across platforms.
The bit: set a slow documentary score, place a chair in frame, walk in and sit down like you're about to speak on the record. On-screen text sets the scene with niche commentary. Low production beats polished content right now.
DOING IT WELL
Niche professionals
Property managers, freelancers using the format to satirize their industries with relatable humor.
Micro-creators (under 500 followers)
Routinely hitting millions of views with performative-distress formats that feel authentic.
Cross-platform Low production Viral format
IG-02
Educational Carousels
Carousels earn 3-5x the save/share rate of single images and are driving the highest engagement in July 2026.
Posts that feel real, have a clear point, and encourage saves or shares outperform polished but generic content. Strong hooks and useful information that matches current audience interests work best. The algorithm prioritizes content that keeps people swiping.
DOING IT WELL
Startup founders
Using step-by-step carousels to build authority and drive saves for tutorial content.
Business accounts
Subtitle Gratitude Format—no licensing required, delivers utility without trending audio dependency.
Saves driver Educational Evergreen
IG-03
Summer Routine Loops
Carefully selected clips showing ideal summer days create smooth loops that encourage viewers to keep watching.
Creators use short clips—morning coffee, workout, pool time, café work—to turn ordinary routines into intentional aesthetics. The format repeats its visual hook several times. Works especially well for lifestyle creators, freelancers, and wellness brands.
DOING IT WELL
Lifestyle creators
Pairing "Over the Top" audio with sun-drenched footage timed to lyric drops for rewatch loops.
Small business owners
Showing "day in the life" summer content with aesthetic behind-the-scenes moments.
Seasonal Rewatch loops Aesthetic
LinkedIn
B2B Content Shifts
LI-01
Document Posts Dominate
Native documents (carousels/PDFs) lead all formats with a 7% engagement rate—more than double video or single images.
The algorithm rewards clickability now more than post frequency. Clicks rose 4.9% year-over-year while posting frequency dropped 10%. LinkedIn tracks every carousel swipe and "see more" click, factoring these invisible interactions into engagement rates.
DOING IT WELL
B2B strategists
Using multi-page carousels with clear frameworks that encourage swipes and shares.
Thought leaders
Packaging insights into PDF-style documents that deliver educational value and drive saves.
Format winner Clickability 7% ER
LI-02
Raw Behind-the-Scenes Video
Unpolished 3-6 minute horizontal clips outlining thought processes are driving higher engagement than produced content.
People are starved for simple, helpful information delivered in non-thought-leadery formats. Raw, behind-the-scenes feeling posts work because they prioritize clarity over production value. Video engagement grew 36% year-over-year despite platform challenges.
DOING IT WELL
Semrush (Julia Holmqvist)
Posting horizontal clips with authentic frameworks showing how their team approaches work.
Founders & operators
Sharing personal lessons and failures in video format to build authentic connection.
36% YoY growth Authenticity Process content
LI-03
Employee-Generated Content
Audiences trust employees more than influencers or CEOs—employee content earns consistently higher engagement because it feels authentic.
Instead of posting from company pages, individual employees share lessons and insights. The 2026 algorithm (360Brew AI) deprioritizes engagement pods and automation but rewards genuine conversations. Employee advocacy is becoming critical for reach.
DOING IT WELL
Sales managers
Sharing lessons from closing difficult deals with real stories that create conversations.
Social teams on camera
Stepping in front of the camera to build personal, human connections with target audiences.
Trust factor Employee advocacy Human connection
YouTube
Long & Short Form Momentum
YT-01
Repeatable Shorts Formats
The fastest path to trending Shorts is choosing formats you can repeat without losing quality—consistency beats one-off virality.
Mini vlogs, food, dance, DIY, and explainers dominate because they're easy to understand, easy to rewatch, and easy to post consistently. Shorts hit 200 billion daily views in 2025. Formats cluster around fast comprehension, retention, and rewatch loops.
DOING IT WELL
Niche tutorial creators
Posting weekly DIY and explainer Shorts that deliver quick value in under 60 seconds.
Food & recipe accounts
Using consistent format structures that viewers instantly recognize and come back for.
200B daily views Repeatable Consistency
YT-02
Event-Driven Authenticity
Viewers reward real people, real reactions, and real-time culture—content tied to live cultural moments outperforms polished production.
Music, gaming, and trailers win because they give people identity, tension, and a reason to come back. Authenticity beat polish in July's viral trends. Direct creator presence, local content, and real-life interaction keep gaining ground.
DOING IT WELL
Reaction & commentary creators
Posting first-person reactions to announcements and events within hours of them breaking.
Convention & event vloggers
Capturing real moments at live events to talk about them later with authentic energy.
Real-time wins Authenticity Event content
YT-03
Faceless AI Channels
Faceless channels running on voiceover, screen recordings, and AI-generated visuals are among the fastest-growing formats in 2026.
The author never appears on camera—everything runs on AI tools, stock footage, or screen captures. This format is scaling because it removes production barriers while maintaining quality. Analytics show which videos drive real subscribers vs. one-off spikes.
DOING IT WELL
Educational explainer channels
Using AI voiceovers and screen recordings to teach complex topics without appearing on camera.
Stock footage + narration accounts
Combining AI-generated visuals with strong scripting for edutainment content that scales.
Fast growth AI production Scalable
