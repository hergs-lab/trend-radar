TikTok

TK-01
Numbered Series Format
Post one themed video every day in October and number it — episodic content brings viewers back daily and converts them to followers.
The '31 Days of Halloween' format shows how serialisation drives retention. A bakery does 31 spooky treats, a bookstore 31 horror picks, a nail salon 31 themed sets. The numbered structure creates anticipation and habit.

TK-02
UGC-Style Creator Content
Branded content that looks and sounds native to the platform clears the first filter that eliminates most brand posts.
UGC is the single best-performing content type on TikTok at 55.7% of top posts. When brands adapt trending formats with a brand-specific spin while maintaining creator aesthetics, they achieve workable engagement rather than being scrolled past.
DOING IT WELL
Brands using creator partnerships
Authentic voice beats polished corporate content
Educational creators in niche verticals
How-to content with trend participation earning reach
Algorithm priority Authenticity Platform-native
TK-03
'Be OK' Contrast Format
Upbeat audio backs a happy dance while on-screen text reveals what's actually going wrong — the contrast makes relatable chaos shareable.
Ingrid Michaelson's track creates the perfect ironic backdrop. Text overlays spell out everyday disasters: sending 'please see attached' with no attachment, drinking one sip all day and calling it hydration. The format works because everyone recognises the performance of being fine.
DOING IT WELL
Creators in relatable content niches
Specific, honest text overlays driving comments and shares
Workplace and student accounts
Professional chaos resonating with office workers and students
Trending audio Relatable Text-on-screen

Instagram

IG-01
Fast-Cut Outfit Transitions
Showcase a week of outfits through 8-15 rapid cuts synced to music beats while repeating the same action in every clip — visual consistency makes each change feel seamless.
The 'Fan Transition' and 'Fall Outfits' trends demonstrate how fast-paced edits with strong hooks in the first two seconds dominate reach. Keeping camera position and movement consistent turns outfit changes into satisfying visual payoff.
DOING IT WELL
Fashion and lifestyle creators
Keyframe animation and masking creating polished transitions

IG-02
Carousel Posts for Trust
Carousels outperform video on saves and shares — they explain process, proof, and decisions in a way followers can save and act on.
While Reels help new people find you, carousels build trust with existing audiences. They achieve 6.60% engagement rate versus 1.80% for video, and drive 2× higher saves. The 'That's So You' identity carousel trend shows how personal specificity resonates.
DOING IT WELL
Educational and business accounts
Step-by-step guides and data visualisations earning saves
Startup founders sharing processes
Searchable content with clear takeaways
High saves Trust builder Educational
IG-03
'Autumn Colors' Seasonal B-Roll
LuLuMusic's relaxed acoustic instrumental has 55K Reels and clears for business accounts — it's the safe pick for seasonal posts because it stays out of the way.
October's cozy fall aesthetic dominates feeds, and this audio provides the backdrop without competing with the visuals. Creators layer it over changing leaves, warm drinks, fall menus, and product shots. It's functional rather than flashy.
DOING IT WELL

LinkedIn

LI-01
Native Document Carousels
Carousels and PDFs lead all formats with 7% engagement rate and 1,451 average impressions per post — far surpassing standard video and single images.
The 2026 benchmarks show a 14% increase in carousel engagement. The algorithm rewards depth and dwell time, and carousels deliver both. Users seeking industry news and career-changing content engage more with skimmable, structured formats than promotional material.
DOING IT WELL
B2B marketers and consultants
Original research and data visualisations earning saves
Thought leaders in niche industries
Step-by-step frameworks packaged in carousel format
Top format High engagement Dwell time
LI-02
Employee-Generated Content
In 2026, people trust people more than company logos — employee posts consistently earn higher engagement than company pages because they feel authentic.
Personal profiles generate more interaction than company pages, and content shared by employees receives higher engagement. A Sales Manager sharing lessons from closing a difficult deal generates more qualified conversations than promotional content from the brand account.
DOING IT WELL
Sales and customer success teams
Real stories from the field building credibility
Founders and CEOs
Behind-the-scenes decision-making resonating with professionals
Authenticity wins Trust signal Personal brand
LI-03
Short, Punchy Text Posts
Posts under 100 words with a strong hook get read more and drive quicker engagement — people scroll quickly, so brevity with substance wins.
The algorithm now measures dwell time, rewarding content that stops the scroll and holds attention. Short text posts often double as authentic, unfiltered thoughts that feel relatable. They outperform long-form when paired with a clear point of view.
DOING IT WELL
Professionals sharing hot takes
Clear opinion with no fluff driving comments and debate
Industry commentators
Single insight posts that spark immediate reaction
Quick read Hook-driven Conversation starter

YouTube

YT-01
Shorts as Discovery Engine
Over 61% of organic views come from the Shorts feed with average viewing time near 16 seconds — open with the problem, claim, or proof immediately, not branding.
Shorts reached 200 billion daily views in 2025 and now drive discovery at scale. The format rewards day-in-the-life mini vlogs, food challenges, quick DIY hacks, and educational explainers. Success depends on complete story in under a minute: context, motion, payoff.
DOING IT WELL
Educational creators (fitness, tech, finance)
Quick lessons with clear steps driving high save rates
Daniel LaBelle, Lucas and Marcus
Visual comedy and stunts earning broad, family-safe engagement
Primary discovery Fast hook 16-sec average
YT-02
Serialised Content
Both extremes work: raw unedited yap videos and highly cinematic production-heavy content — the common thread is serialisation that makes people come back for the next episode.
Platforms reward recurring content series. 'Part 1, Part 2' videos, daily themed content, and numbered series drive higher follower conversion. Brands are creating series that mirror TV shows, like Office-style workplace content, because audiences want to follow along.
DOING IT WELL
Narrative and true crime channels
Story arcs with beginning, middle, end in 60-90 seconds
Brands with workplace series
Episode-based content building recurring viewership
Recurring audience Episode format Retention
YT-03
Educational Micro-Content
'Learn something in 60 seconds' videos drive high save rates and repeat views — creators in education space are seeing fastest growth rates on every platform.
Topics range from science facts to financial literacy to technology explainers. The TikTok and YouTube algorithms both favour this format because it delivers value quickly. Viewers save content for later and rewatch, signalling quality to the algorithm.
