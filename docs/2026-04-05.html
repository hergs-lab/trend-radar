TikTok

TK-01

Reali-Tea: Unfiltered Behind-the-Scenes

People are craving grounding through raw, unpolished stories over curated perfection.
After a year of escapism, audiences are shifting toward honesty and real emotional validation. Brands showing real process, people, and BTS moments resonate most. The algorithm rewards authentic storytelling that helps audiences feel present, not distracted.

Authenticity Community BTS
TK-02

"Oh Ok Because" — 212 Wordplay Format

The 212 instrumental meets addictive wordplay in one of the most viral formats of 2026 so far.
Creators do a full-body box step while on-screen text starts with "oh ok because" followed by a broken-up word or phrase (e.g., "oh ok because pay has a day"). Dead simple format, endlessly creative, and highly replicable for brands. The sound is climbing fast.
Doing it well
Trend-first creators on FYP
Early adopters combining physical movement with clever wordplay for instant engagement
Comedy + Dance Hybrid Accounts
Using the format to showcase personality while keeping it accessible for all skill levels
Viral Format Music Trend Dance
TK-03

Niche Community Discovery

Search journeys on TikTok are uncovering unexpected niche audiences with powerful brand affinity.
Duracell discovered K-pop fans as a core audience through TikTok search data—fans use their batteries for concert light sticks. These tight-knit, interest-driven communities punch above their weight in influence. The algorithm increasingly surfaces adjacent spaces where brands can authentically connect.
Doing it well
Duracell
Traced search data to find K-pop community connection, making it a 2026 growth priority
Brands Using TikTok Search Insights
Monitoring organic mentions and adjacent spaces to discover authentic audience overlap
Search Behavior Community Discovery
Platform

Instagram

IG-01

Text Line Drop with Movement

Each jump or stomp reveals a new line of text, turning storytelling into something surprisingly addictive.
This trend keeps viewers engaged longer than static text by making each movement unlock part of a story. It's clean, easy to recreate, and works especially well for opinions, mini-stories, and punchy explanations that build toward a final payoff. Frame shifts happen during editing to reveal hidden text.
Doing it well
Service & Product Brands
Using dynamic motion to highlight features and benefits in a way that stops the scroll
Lifestyle Creators
Building narrative tension through layered reveals that invite viewers to watch until the end
Reels Format Storytelling Visual Hook
IG-02

Harry Styles "Aperture" Sound Wave

Aperture has become the go-to audio for aesthetic, unhurried GRWM and day-in-the-life content.
Harry Styles' lead single from Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally. is driving cinematic, soft-toned Reels. It's versatile, easy to adapt, and instantly recognizable—perfect for both creators and brands. The full album (released March 6) has unlocked 11 more potential trending audios for spring content.
Doing it well
Lifestyle & Fashion Creators
Pairing the calming sound with slow transitions and aesthetic spring content
Beauty & Wellness Accounts
Using it as backdrop for morning routines and self-care moment storytelling
Trending Audio GRWM Aesthetic
IG-03

Nostalgic Filter Comeback

Vintage aesthetics, 90s edits, and Y2K throwbacks are making a strong comeback across Reels.
Creators are using grainy filters, VHS effects, and old-school music to evoke nostalgia. This trend speaks to Millennials and Gen Z who grew up during the 90s and early 2000s. Throwback music enhances the emotional connection, taking viewers back to a familiar time. It's resonating because it feels authentic, not overly polished.
Doing it well
Y2K Fashion & Beauty Creators
Replicating older video formats with filters that give authentic retro vibes
Music & Pop Culture Accounts
Adding classic 90s soundtracks and POV storytelling to deepen nostalgic relatability
Nostalgia Filters Gen Z Appeal
Platform

LinkedIn

LI-01

Native Document Carousels Dominate

Native document posts (PDF carousels) generate the highest engagement on LinkedIn, outperforming images and video.
Carousels boast a 45.85% engagement rate with over 791 average interactions—algorithmic gold because they increase "dwell time." Users swipe through, forcing longer engagement. While multi-image posts get likes, document posts drive meaningful interaction. The format is ideal for sharing insights, data, and educational content that positions you as a thought leader.
Doing it well
B2B Thought Leaders
Repurposing blog posts and reports into visually engaging swipe-through stories
Educational Content Creators
Using structured arcs and dramatic reveals to create tension across slides
Carousel Dwell Time Thought Leadership
LI-02

Raw, Unpolished Behind-the-Scenes

Raw, unpolished behind-the-scenes posts are contributing to higher engagement as audiences crave substance over polish.
Horizontal 3-6 minute clips outlining a thought process or framework are outperforming produced content. People are starved for simple, helpful information delivered in non-"thought leadery" style. Authenticity beats production value—audiences trust content that feels real and human, not corporate.
Doing it well
Founders & CEOs
Sharing personal insights and company news that humanize the brand and build trust
Service Professionals
Posting unscripted process videos and frameworks that demonstrate expertise casually
Authenticity Video BTS
LI-03

Interactive Polls & Engagement Farming

Interactive posts like polls and "comment for access" posts are proving rewarding as engagement and clicks increase.
LinkedIn interactions increased 24% and clicks rose 28% year-over-year. Users are taking advantage by posting polls and lead magnet offers that ask users to comment with a keyword. The algorithm sees engagement spike, pushing the post further. It's also effective for market research and gathering audience insights while building community.
Doing it well
B2B Marketers
Using "comment 'template' for access" posts that boost engagement while building email lists
Consultants & Coaches
Running polls on topical industry questions to spark discussion and position authority
Polls Engagement Lead Gen
Platform

YouTube

YT-01

Serialized, TV-Style Content Formats

Creators are embracing traditional, TV-style formats—serialized videos that give viewers recurring storylines and structure.
YouTube's trend report shows creators building sprawling shared universes and episodic content. Formats like First We Feast's "Hot Ones" have grown from 10-minute slots to hour-long episodes. This nostalgia-driven trend appeals to both older viewers and younger audiences seeking fresh ways to engage. It's a halfway house between sitcom and game.
Doing it well
First We Feast (Hot Ones)
Celebrity interview format with spicy wings—light-hearted conversations with recurring structure
Brazilian Creator Houses
Building interconnected storylines across channels, encouraging cross-platform viewing
Long-Form Serialized Nostalgia
YT-02

YouTube Shorts as Discovery Funnel

Shorts crossed 70 billion daily views and now act as a discovery mechanism feeding long-form content and subscriptions.
Creators earn $0.03-$0.10 per 1,000 Shorts views—far less than long-form's ~$3 RPM—but the volume and funnel potential is massive. Shorts that tease or clip from longer videos drive viewers to full content. YouTube's unique advantage is that short-form directly feeds into a long-form monetization ecosystem.
Doing it well
Educational YouTubers
Using Shorts to preview longer tutorials, turning curiosity into channel subscriptions
Commentary & Essay Channels
Clipping high-impact moments from long videos to attract new viewers to full episodes
Shorts Discovery Funnel
YT-03

Authentic, Unscripted Storytelling

Over-produced videos now often underperform as viewers crave raw, unscripted, and human content that feels real.
Content that feels authentic drives greater trust and engagement. Creators are empowering teams to self-record updates from the field, capturing genuine conversation and behind-the-scenes context. Real faces are in high demand—bad news for voiceover-only channels. Audiences reward creators who go deeper than surface level with intentional, substantive storytelling.
