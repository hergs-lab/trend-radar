TikTok
Reali-tea, companion content & quiet flex aesthetics
TK-01
Reali-Tea: Unfiltered Over Polished
Audiences are moving away from fantasy toward raw, in-progress storytelling and honest behind-the-scenes moments.
TikTok's 2026 Next report identifies "Reali-Tea" as a dominant shift, with users craving authenticity over perfection. The algorithm now rewards unfiltered stories and real people over curated content, reflected in trending hashtags like #lockedin (648K posts) and #joblife (235K posts).

TK-02
Companion Content: With Me Formats
People want company through their screens — "study with me", "get ready with me" and "work a 9-5 with me" formats pull massive engagement.
These lo-fi, unpolished videos resonate because they feel real and relatable. The format creates a parasocial sense of presence that keeps viewers watching longer. Completion rates are highest at 60-90 seconds, though long-form (10+ minutes) also performs on the platform.

TK-03
Quiet Flex Aesthetic
Loud, flashy content is out — TikTok is obsessed with softer, aspirational aesthetics built on calm, cozy lighting and intentionality.
The "quiet flex" features neutral color palettes, clean spaces, morning routines filmed in golden hour and slow-motion coffee pours. This trend counters content fatigue from hyper-edited videos. Alo Yoga exemplifies this with serene stretches and minimalist studio shots that feel elevated without being overproduced.

Instagram
YAP format, text-line reveals & audio-driven brand moments
IG-01
YAP Format: Raw Talking-Head Reels
Minimally edited selfie videos with subtitles and text hooks are crushing it — countering over-edited content and fake AI by being genuinely human.
The so-called "YAP format" has become popular in personal brand spaces. These are off-the-cuff, no-script videos with minimal editing. Max Tornow has amassed over 800,000 views on Reels using this format. The trend reflects audience demand for less polished, more authentic content that feels real.
Doing It Well
Max Tornow
Entrepreneur using raw selfie videos with minimal editing to deliver business insights
Personal brand creators
Fast-turn opinion content that feels like a conversation, not a commercial
Authenticity Low-production Selfie-style
IG-02
Text Line Drop: Motion-Revealed Storytelling
Each jump or stomp reveals a new line of text, turning simple messages into surprisingly addictive, dynamic storytelling.
This format keeps viewers engaged longer than static text posts by making even short messages feel dynamic and intentional. It works especially well for opinions, mini-stories and punchy explanations that build toward a payoff. The motion and progressive reveal create anticipation.
Doing It Well
Brand & creator accounts
Using physical movement to reveal product benefits or story beats step-by-step
Opinion creators
Building arguments or lists line-by-line with satisfying visual rhythm
High completion Progressive reveal Easy to replicate
IG-03
Justin Bieber "EVERYTHING HALLELUJAH"
After his Coachella comeback, brands are listing wins or features one by one, each followed by "hallelujah" — creating a satisfying rhythm.
The audio went straight to the top of Instagram's trending list in May. The repetition creates punctuation, making even ordinary lists feel like celebrations. Visit Austin used it for city highlights ("live music capital of the world, hallelujah"). The format is simple: 5-8 things your brand does well, then add "hallelujah" after each.

LinkedIn
Document posts dominate, founder voices & dwell time over likes
LI-01
Native Documents: 6.6% Engagement Leader
PDF carousels and native document posts are hitting the highest engagement rates of any LinkedIn format — more than double standard text posts.
Socialinsider data shows native documents scoring 7% engagement with 14% YoY increase. Carousels earn 1,451 average impressions per post versus 605 for video. The format works because it encourages clicks, swipes and dwell time — signals the algorithm heavily rewards in 2026. Multi-image posts also outperform at 3.71% engagement.
Doing It Well
Educational brands & consultants
Repurposing blog content into carousel format with clear frameworks
Thought leaders
Using step-by-step guides and data stories in swipeable formats
Highest engagement Dwell time Saves
LI-02
Founder & Personal Brand Content
Personal profiles drive 2.6% engagement vs 1.6% for company pages — people trust faces over faceless brands.
LinkedIn data shows personal profiles generate more interactions and post more frequently. The shift reflects audiences seeking genuine conversation over corporate messaging. Founder content — CEOs and founders sharing insights, perspectives and company news — adds human touch and builds trust. This trend continues to strengthen in 2026.

LI-03
Algorithm Shift: Dwell Time is King
LinkedIn prioritizes how long people engage with content over quick likes — 30 seconds of reading beats 50 fast likes.
The 2026 algorithm shifted from viral reach to "Depth and Authority." Posts driving dwell time get pushed to more feeds. External links see ~60% less reach. The system detects "click bounces" and deprioritizes content. Saves are 5x more valuable than likes. Comment quality now trumps quantity — 20 thoughtful comments beat 200 emoji reactions.
Doing It Well

Crafting hooks and narratives that keep readers on the post for 30+ seconds
Data-driven thought leaders
Sharing original frameworks that spark meaningful discussion in comments
Dwell time priority Quality > quantity Saves matter
YouTube
TV-style serialization, Shorts maturity & recurring formats
YT-01
Serialized, Episodic Content
Viewers crave structure and recurring formats — episodic series keep audiences coming back while random one-off clips are becoming table stakes.
YouTube's 2026 Culture & Trends Report shows creators embracing TV-style formats. Druski's "Coulda Been" franchise (House, Love spin-offs) attracted 710K viewers. Quenlin Blackwell's "feeding starving celebrities" series became a humor-driven interview format. The structure builds anticipation and loyalty, with limited-episode runs preventing viewer fatigue.

Glitch Studio (Glitch Direct)
Behind-the-scenes vlog format previewing upcoming animated series in transparent, casual style
Binge appeal Recurring formats TV-inspired
YT-02
Shorts-First Storytelling
YouTube Shorts receive over 90 billion views daily — the format has matured into a powerful discovery layer for creators and brands.
Shorts under 60 seconds require condensed, impactful storytelling with clear hooks in the first 3 seconds. The vertical 9:16 format (1080×1920px) is optimized for mobile-first viewing. Successful creators publish across multiple formats: Shorts for discovery, long-form for depth, and community posts for ongoing engagement. Multi-format publishing creates more recommendation opportunities.

Multi-format creators

90B daily views Discovery layer Mobile-first
YT-03
Video Podcasts & Long-Form Depth
YouTube is capturing both streaming eyeballs in living rooms and creator culture influence — long-form watch sessions are growing.
Video podcasts continue to grow as YouTube strengthens its position in long-form content. Audiences seek longer watch sessions, personality-led content and expert perspectives. The platform rewards viewer satisfaction over follower count, with longer immersive formats thriving on TV screens. Brands benefit from dual strategy: Shorts for mobile discovery, long-form for TV depth.
