Platform 01
TikTok
Realism beats fantasy, curiosity-driven discovery, emotional ROI verification
TK-01
Delayed-Realisation Hindsight
Creators surface memories they completely missed at the time — the sound lands at the exact moment it clicks.
This format runs on hindsight using the "Yartrix" original sound. Versions that travel are specific and slightly embarrassing — realising someone was throwing shade, missing obvious signals, not understanding workplace dynamics until years later. The delayed-realisation framing lets brands tell the joke without pointing at customers.
Doing it well
Hospitality & service brands
Every industry has something everybody quietly understood except one person — perfect for inside-joke storytelling
Workplace culture accounts
Hindsight about office dynamics, intern mistakes, first-job naivety hits hard with professional audiences
Original Sound Storytelling Relatability
TK-02
My American Girl Doll Energy
Creators reveal grown-up prized possessions with unfiltered childhood-level joy — the mismatch is the joke.
Angelo David resurfaced a 2009 childhood clip introducing his American Girl doll with full excitement. The audio became a format: reveal something you just acquired with disproportionate enthusiasm. A package that landed yesterday, a diploma mentioned constantly, a best friend met last week. Works because the contrast between childhood-level hype and mundane adult acquisition is instantly funny.
Doing it well
Angelo David (@angelodavid)
Originated the trend with authentic childhood footage — set the template for mismatch humour
Product launch brands
Perfect for unboxing content that leans into over-the-top excitement without feeling forced
Viral Audio Nostalgia Humour
TK-03
Daily/Weekly/Monthly Routine Splits
Creators split habits into time buckets — skincare, posting schedules, training — a format simple enough to slot into any niche.
This educational format breaks routines down by frequency: what I do daily, weekly, monthly. Works across fitness, beauty, productivity, content creation. The structure is so clear it removes creative friction — audiences instantly understand the framework. Brands use it for product ecosystems (daily serum, weekly mask, monthly facial) or content strategies (daily stories, weekly posts, monthly campaigns).
Doing it well
Beauty & skincare creators
Natural fit for product layering, frequency-based routines, and before/after storytelling
Productivity & business accounts
Time-boxing content, habit stacking, and workflow optimisation all map to this structure perfectly
Educational Structure Evergreen
Platform 02
Instagram
DM shares outrank likes, carousels at 0.50% engagement vs Reels at 0.48%
IG-01
Potential-Maxxing with Hard Numbers
Creators back up self-improvement claims with data — post counts, view totals, measurable progress — instead of vague affirmations.
This trend rejects aspirational fluff and demands proof. Creators show screenshots of analytics, post frequency, follower growth curves, revenue milestones. The shift reflects audience fatigue with motivational content that lacks substance. Uses original audio so business accounts can adopt it. Perfect for before/after transformations where the numbers tell the story more convincingly than the caption.
Doing it well
Business coaches & consultants
Client results, revenue proof, measurable transformations build trust faster than testimonials alone
Fitness & wellness creators
Weight lifted, miles run, habit streaks — all quantifiable proof that progress is real, not performative
Authenticity Data-Driven Results
IG-02
Age-Anchored Wisdom Hooks
Your age becomes the hook — "things I know at 27" instantly targets your bracket and offers a window into the future for everyone younger.
This format leverages relatability and curiosity in one move. People in your age group feel seen, younger audiences get a preview of what's coming, older audiences compare notes. The algorithm rewards it because the age number pre-qualifies the audience — people self-select into engagement. Works for life lessons, career pivots, financial milestones, relationship realisations.
Doing it well
Career & personal development creators
Age-specific career advice (promotions at 30, pivots at 35) feels more credible than generic guidance
Lifestyle & finance accounts
Money lessons learned at specific ages resonate because the timeline makes the advice feel earned, not taught
Hook Strategy Relatability Life Stages
IG-03
Manifesting with Dua Lipa's "Training Season"
Creators manifest what's coming their way — new goals, opportunities, lifestyles — acting like it's already inevitable.
Using Dua Lipa's "Training Season" audio, creators show themselves doing something that matches what they're calling in. The fun is in the confidence: treating aspirations like foregone conclusions. Text overlay says what they're manifesting. Works as both sincere (genuine goals) and ironic (absurdly specific desires). Brands use it to show product integration as part of the manifested life.
Doing it well
Lifestyle & travel creators
Aspirational content feels earned when framed as manifestation, not flex — aligns with audience desires
UGC & brand partnership creators
Product integration within manifested lifestyle scenarios feels organic, not transactional
Licensed Audio Aspirational Mindset
Platform 03
LinkedIn
Clicks matter more than likes, carousels dominate reach, comment quality weighted 3x higher
LI-01
Native Document Carousels
Native PDFs and carousels lead all formats with 7% engagement — double the rate of video or single images.
LinkedIn's algorithm heavily rewards carousels because every swipe registers as a click, boosting invisible engagement metrics that determine reach. The format supports both educational content and storytelling, working across funnel stages. Multi-image posts hit 3.71% engagement vs 1.80% for video. Plain language beats corporate tone in 2026 — authenticity outperforms polish.
Doing it well
B2B SaaS & professional services
How-to guides, frameworks, and case study breakdowns all benefit from swipeable, saveable structure
Thought leaders & consultants
Multi-slide insights position expertise while generating clicks, saves, and shares that amplify reach
High Engagement Algorithm-Favoured Educational
LI-02
Substantive Comment Threads
Posts with meaningful comment threads (5+ words per comment) receive up to 3x more distribution than single-word reactions.
LinkedIn's September 2026 algorithm update prioritises comment quality over vanity metrics. A comment is now treated as a trust signal — the platform interprets multi-sentence replies as genuine conversation, not hollow engagement bait. Posts that ask genuine questions or invite debate trigger deeper responses. One thoughtful comment outweighs ten fire emojis for reach.
Doing it well
Industry analysts & commentators
Hot takes on trends, contrarian POVs, and open-ended questions naturally spark multi-paragraph debates
CEOs & founders sharing lessons
Personal stories about failures, pivots, and decisions invite others to share their own experiences
Algorithm Shift Conversation Quality Engagement
LI-03
Polls & Interactive Formats
Interactive content — polls, LinkedIn Lives, co-hosted events — generates discussion and is rewarded with higher reach.
LinkedIn is for networking, not broadcasting. Posts that invite participation align with platform intent and are algorithmically favoured. Polls are quick to create and engage with, often generating comment threads. Collaborations (co-hosted Lives, challenges, expert interviews) tap into partner networks, multiplying reach. One comment increases the chance someone sees your next post by 80%.
Doing it well
Marketing & HR leaders
Polls on industry trends, workforce strategy, and tools generate participation and valuable market intelligence
Event organisers & educators
LinkedIn Lives and webinar replays drive real-time interaction and private conversation initiation
Participation Networking Collaboration
Platform 04
YouTube
Shorts hit 200B daily views, mixed formats win discovery + retention, completion rate is king
YT-01
Short-to-Long Funnel Strategy
Shorts win discovery, long-form builds trust and buyer action — the winning move is using both formats together.
YouTube Shorts reached 200 billion daily views in 2025, reshaping creator discovery. But retention and conversion happen in longer content. Smart creators use Shorts to spark interest, then move viewers to demos, explainers, live sessions, or product walkthroughs. The format mix addresses different viewer needs: quick entertainment vs deep education. Madeline Argy and others are jumping from short-form to long-form to capture both.
Doing it well
Education & how-to channels
Shorts tease the problem, long-form delivers the full solution — natural progression keeps audiences engaged
Product & SaaS brands
Quick Shorts demo one feature, long walkthroughs show the full workflow and answer buyer objections
Format Mix Discovery Conversion
YT-02
Experiment & Challenge Formats
Viewers want to know the outcome — hypothesis, experiment, process, result — challenges remain a powerful viral tool.
The experiment format has a built-in retention hook: the viewer needs to know how it ends. "I lived for a week spending only $10 a day — here's what happened" beats "5 ways to save money" because it creates narrative tension. The structure is simple: establish stakes, show the process, reveal results. You don't need to copy challenges exactly — adapt the format to your niche.
Doing it well
IShowSpeed (@ishowspeed)
Gained 11-17M subs in a year through meme-driven challenges — cultural relevance meets repeatable format
Lifestyle & productivity creators
Budget experiments, habit tests, productivity challenges all benefit from clear before/after storytelling
Retention Hook Storytelling Viral Potential
YT-03
Creator Crossover Universes
Sprawling shared universes — collaborations across channels and platforms — make viewers feel part of the story.
Brazil's top YouTubers build interconnected storylines across creator houses, requiring viewers to follow multiple channels to piece together beefs, developments, and narratives. The crossover appeal makes audiences feel invested — they need to watch everything to stay current. This format works for brand ecosystems too: different products, different creators, one connected story world.
Doing it well
Brazilian YouTube creator houses
Multi-creator storylines across channels build completionist viewing behaviour and sustained engagement
Gaming & entertainment collectives
Shared universes, recurring characters, and cross-channel narratives keep audiences coming back for lore
Collaboration Storytelling Community
