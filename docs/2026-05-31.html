TikTok
What's Breaking Through
TK-01
"Self Aware" Audio Revival
Aesthetic montages + thoughtful text overlays are exploding again — best time to jump in if you missed April's first wave.
Creators pair b-roll with emotional, slightly dramatic, or empowering text using the "Self Aware" audio by Temper City. The format is flexible: can feel calm, confident, nostalgic, or like a soft-life montage depending on clips and messaging. Perfect for lifestyle, wellness, and product storytelling.
DOING IT WELL
@abbeyequestrian
Uses nostalgic animal content with heartfelt text overlays to drive emotional connection.
Wellness & lifestyle creators
Showing calm, put-together life moments or transformation narratives with the audio.
AESTHETIC TRENDING AUDIO FLEXIBLE
TK-02
"And Emily… That's All" Judgment Format
The Devil Wears Prada 2 revived Miranda Priestly energy — subtle judgment contrasts are charting across fashion, work, and lifestyle niches.
This trend uses the iconic "and Emily… that's all" audio to compare two contrasting setups or approaches, with one side polished and the other chaotic. The humor comes from the calm, unimpressed delivery. Works for before/after, tool comparisons, or product vs. no-product scenarios. Still at peak as of mid-May.
DOING IT WELL
Fashion & beauty creators
Using the format to show outfit glow-ups or makeup transformations with editorial flair.
Productivity & SaaS brands
Contrasting messy workflows vs. streamlined tool-based solutions with deadpan humor.
FILM TIE-IN COMPARISON HUMOR
TK-03
Fake World Cup Squads
Creators are drafting absurdist fantasy football teams featuring pop-culture icons, politicians, and inanimate objects ahead of the World Cup.
Almost time for kick-off, and TikTok is having fun building squads that go far beyond actual footballers. The trend mixes sports passion with comedy, allowing for creative storytelling and cultural commentary. It's shareable, generates debate in comments, and taps into the global World Cup hype cycle.
DOING IT WELL
@itsammarbrzi
Went viral drafting a Bosnian squad featuring a can of chicken pâté — absurdist humor at its best.
Sports & meme creators
Blending football knowledge with pop-culture references to build shareable, debate-worthy lineups.

Instagram
Reels & Carousels Driving Reach
IG-01
"You're So Creative" Behind-the-Scenes
Creators are showing polished work followed by the chaotic reality behind it — millennial audiences keep responding to creative process confessionals.
The format stitches polished final work with "You're so creative!" overlay, then cuts to honest, messy behind-the-scenes footage. It's relatable, humanizing, and resonates especially with designers, photographers, editors, and freelancers. At 130K+ Reels, it's at peak — act fast if using it.
DOING IT WELL
Design studios & photographers
Showing the 200 takes, chaotic desk setups, and midnight deadlines behind polished client work.
Content creators & social managers
Revealing the 47 takes behind the perfect Reel — making the process feel real and relatable.
BTS RELATABLE MILLENNIAL
IG-02
Carousel Photo Dumps with Intentional Sequencing
Photo dumps are evolving — creators are treating carousels like visual stories with emotional arcs instead of random snapshots.
Instagram now allows reordering carousel slides after posting, removing a major pain point. Creators are using this to craft intentional sequences mixing selfies, scenery, food, candid moments, and memes in a narrative flow. It's driving consistent follower growth without needing mega-viral reach.
DOING IT WELL
Lifestyle & travel creators
Building emotional journeys through intentional slide ordering — not just random photo collections.
Personal brand accounts
Using carousel storytelling to build deeper connection with audiences over time.
CAROUSEL STORYTELLING GROWTH
IG-03
Calvin Klein x Jung Kook Campaign Format
A single Reel hit 44.7M views — the strategy is worth studying: minimal branding, cinematic visuals, feels like a fan edit instead of a traditional ad.
Calvin Klein's CKJK capsule campaign is functioning as organic content. No product specs, just cinematic visuals of the ambassador in the clothes. The caption: "discover on CalvinKlein.com" and nothing else. This creator-native approach drove 150x their normal engagement — a masterclass in branded content that doesn't feel like an ad.
DOING IT WELL
@calvinklein
Making high-budget campaigns feel like native fan content — minimal text, maximum vibe.
Fashion & lifestyle brands
Shifting from product-heavy ads to cinematic, ambassador-led storytelling that feels organic.

LinkedIn
What's Earning Trust & Engagement
LI-01
Native Carousels Dominating Engagement
Carousels get 17x more interactions than images yet are posted 6x less — the format is massively underutilized and drives 7% engagement rate.
Native document posts (PDFs/carousels) lead all formats with a 7% engagement rate, up 14% year-over-year. They increase dwell time, encourage saves and shares, and signal quality to the algorithm. The bar has risen: generic Canva templates don't cut it anymore — winning carousels have original insights, clean typography, and clear narrative arcs.
DOING IT WELL
B2B thought leaders
Using carousels for frameworks, case study breakdowns, and data-backed insights with professional design.
Marketing & SaaS founders
Breaking down complex concepts into swipeable slides that get saved and shared widely.
CAROUSEL HIGH ENG. UNDERUSED
LI-02
Clicks Are the "Invisible" Ranking Signal
Clicks per post rose 4.9% year-over-year — LinkedIn tracks every carousel swipe and "see more" tap, and these matter more than likes for reach.
Engagement rates jumped from 12.21% to 13.90% even as posting frequency dropped 10%. Quality and "clickability" now matter more than volume. Posts that encourage interaction — carousels, polls, external links — perform better because LinkedIn rewards dwell time and user action, not just passive scrolling.
DOING IT WELL
Data-driven marketers
Using hooks that prompt "see more" clicks and carousel swipes to boost algorithmic distribution.
Consultants & coaches
Crafting posts with strong openings that require a click to reveal the full insight or framework.
ALGORITHM DWELL TIME QUALITY > QTY
LI-03
Authenticity Over Polish
Personal profiles with authentic stories get dramatically more reach than polished corporate messaging — audiences trust people more than brands.
Thought leadership from real voices is outperforming corporate updates. 85% of FTSE CEOs now use LinkedIn (up from 12% in 2023), signaling that leaders are taking personal positioning seriously. Posts blending personal experience with professional insight, original frameworks, and transparent storytelling drive the most trust and shares.
DOING IT WELL
Founders & CEOs
Sharing lessons learned, decision-making processes, and behind-the-scenes leadership insights authentically.
Industry experts & consultants
Building authority through transparent, experience-based storytelling rather than generic corporate speak.

YouTube
Formats & Franchises Building Loyalty
YT-01
Serialized, Recurring Formats
Viewers prefer channels with structured arcs and recurring shows — one-off clips are table stakes, but series build loyalty and anticipation.
Creators like Quenlin Blackwell ("Feeding Starving Celebrities") and Druski ("Coulda Been House") are turning formats into franchises. Episodes run under 10 parts for binge appeal without fatigue. Serialized content drives repeat views, subscription stickiness, and community building — it's TV thinking applied to YouTube.
DOING IT WELL
Quenlin Blackwell
"Feeding Starving Celebrities" evolved from 10-min to hour-long episodes with consistent format and charm.
Druski
Built a reality franchise ("Coulda Been" series) with raw improv energy and limited-series structure.
SERIES RECURRING LOYALTY
YT-02
Long-Form Comeback
After years of Shorts dominance, watch time on 10+ minute videos is growing again — channels mixing Shorts for discovery with long-form for retention are winning.
Shorts drive reach and discovery. Long-form drives watch time, ad revenue, and audience depth. The strongest 2026 channels publish both. Creators like Madeline Argy (40+ minute reflective essays) and Gary's Economics (30-min explainers) are proving audiences still crave depth when the content delivers genuine value.
DOING IT WELL
Madeline Argy
Built Gen Z following with raw 40+ minute reflections on feminism, imposter syndrome, and relationships.
Gary's Economics
30-minute explainers on inflation and inequality with stripped-down visuals — focused, not flashy.
LONG-FORM DEPTH MONETIZATION
YT-03
Behind-the-Scenes Studio Formats
Glitch Studio's "Glitch Direct" blurs the line between creator and studio — fans feel included in the production pipeline, building anticipation for launches.
Glitch (known for The Amazing Digital Circus) launched a behind-the-scenes format where founders preview upcoming series in a casual vlog style. It's transparent, community-driven, and turns the studio itself into a character. This direct-to-fan approach builds trust, anticipation, and emotional investment in the content pipeline.
DOING IT WELL
Glitch Studio
Making fans feel like insiders by showing previews, production updates, and founder commentary casually.
Animation & game studios
Using YouTube as a direct pipeline to fans, bypassing traditional media gatekeepers entirely.
