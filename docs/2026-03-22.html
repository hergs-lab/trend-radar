TikTok
March on TikTok
TK-01
"Boom Clap" Moment of Being Seen
Small asks that get surprisingly positive responses create deeply relatable micro-wins people want to share.
Creators share a casual comment they made expecting to be ignored, then reveal the unexpectedly validating reply. The format pairs Charli XCX's "Boom Clap" with text overlays showing the original statement and the response that made them feel seen.

@deviyabha

Dating & wellness brands
Framing products as the "unexpected positive response" to audience needs

TK-02
Admin Night: Making Adulting Social
Reframing boring tasks as cozy social events taps into desire to make mundane responsibilities feel less isolating.
Young adults film themselves tackling bills, emails, and planning with friends in aesthetically pleasing setups—laptops, snacks, planners, soft lighting. The trend emerged early 2026 as a coping mechanism for high inflation and overwhelming admin tasks.
DOING IT WELL
@maddyagers
Frames admin nights as "making bureaucracy sexy" with hashtag campaigns
Productivity tool brands
Showing products in "admin night" context rather than corporate settings
Lifestyle Gen Z Aesthetic
TK-03
365 Buttons: "It Only Has to Make Sense to Me"
Personal systems that seem illogical to others become badges of authentic individuality and self-permission.
Creator @laniekristin went viral with 365 buttons moved between jars daily. Her response—"it only has to make sense to me"—became a 2026 mantra. Creators adapt the format with screenshots, tabs, drafts, or other personal tracking systems that defy explanation.
DOING IT WELL

Wellness & productivity brands
Selling systems not outcomes, celebrating idiosyncratic approaches

Childhood photos paired with current job titles humanize brands and make team intros feel playful rather than corporate.
Brands share employees' childhood photos with text overlay: "This is who you're asking to [job function]." The contrast between adorable kid photos and serious professional roles generates warmth and shareability while introducing the humans behind the brand.
DOING IT WELL

Using format to make technical expertise feel approachable and trustworthy
Agencies & creative studios
Showcasing team personality to differentiate from competitors

Harry Styles "Aperture" Cinematic Storytelling
Natural audio build enables reflective, polished storytelling without feeling overproduced—mood over punchlines.
Harry Styles' "Aperture" from Kiss All the Time album has become go-to sound for meaningful clips, recaps, and visually satisfying moments. The track's build makes it perfect for soft transitions and moments that feel worth pausing on, working equally well for personal and brand content.

Professional Authenticity Wins
LI-01
Native Documents Claim Top Engagement
Carousel PDFs maximize dwell time and signal thought leadership, earning 7.00% engagement rate—14% YoY increase.
LinkedIn's algorithm heavily rewards content that keeps users on-platform. Multi-page carousels require active clicking, generating extended interaction signals that trigger broader distribution. Brands repurpose blog posts, case studies, and frameworks into swipeable documents.
DOING IT WELL
B2B thought leaders

Agencies & consultants

LI-02
Raw, Unpolished Behind-the-Scenes Video
3-6 minute horizontal clips outlining thought processes feel simple and helpful, outperforming polished productions.
LinkedIn audiences are starved for practical, straightforward information. Raw video content showing frameworks, processes, or work approaches—without "thought leader" theatrics—is driving strong engagement. The format respects professional context while feeling authentic.
DOING IT WELL
Founders & operators
Sharing real decision-making frameworks without corporate polish
B2B service providers
Walking through client challenges in one-take explanatory videos
Authenticity Educational Video
LI-03
Lead Magnet Comments for Engagement Farming
Posts offering templates/guides via comment keyword drive algorithm-friendly engagement spikes and qualified lead capture.
The "Comment 'X' for access" format exploded in late 2025 as LinkedIn interactions increased 24% YoY. The mechanic generates rapid comment velocity, signaling value to the algorithm while building a list of engaged contacts. Works best with genuinely useful resources.
DOING IT WELL
Marketing consultants
Offering frameworks and templates that solve real professional pain points
SaaS brands
Using lead magnets to identify and nurture qualified prospects in comments

YouTube
Long-Form + Shorts Dual Strategy
YT-01
Serialized TV-Style Content Returns
Episodic formats and recurring series build loyalty and binge-ability, appealing to TV-screen viewers seeking depth.
YouTube creators are embracing traditional TV formats with serialized storylines, recurring segments, and multi-episode arcs. This shift leverages increasing TV viewership (YouTube Shorts get 90B daily views but long-form drives deeper relationships). Audiences prefer structured, recurring content they can follow.
DOING IT WELL
Druski (Coulda Been House)
Big Brother-style reality format with limited series structure for digestibility
Educational channels
Multi-part explainer series that reward consistent viewership
Long-Form Bingeable Loyalty
YT-02
Authentic, Unfiltered Content Over Production
Raw, meandering content builds intimacy—simplified thumbnails, minimal editing, and sincere storytelling outperform polish.
Creators like Sam Sulek and Dry Creek Wrangler School demonstrate that over-produced content now underperforms. Audiences crave authentic, unscripted moments that feel human. Simpler thumbnails, relaxed pacing, and genuine personality create stronger connections than high production values.
DOING IT WELL
Sam Sulek
Bodybuilder filming uncut workouts with authentic commentary, no polish
Dry Creek Wrangler School
Horse channel pivoted to uncut life lessons with genuine storytelling
Authenticity Low-Fi Trust-Building
YT-03
Shorts as Discovery, Long-Form for Depth
Shorts drive new audience discovery quickly while long-form builds loyalty—successful creators use both strategically.
YouTube's dual format strategy is maturing: Shorts act as trailers and entry points (90B daily views), while long-form videos (8-20 minutes) create depth and monetization. Creators repurpose highlights into Shorts, then direct viewers to long-form content for full context and community.
DOING IT WELL
Multi-format creators
Using Shorts for hooks, long-form for comprehensive storytelling and revenue
Brands with educational content
Shorts for quick tips, long-form for tutorials and deeper value
Multi-Format Discovery Strategic
