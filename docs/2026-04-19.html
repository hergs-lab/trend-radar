TikTok
Audio-driven identity, glow-up energy, and self-aware storytelling
April's TikTok trends lean into fresh-start momentum with reflective audio formats, transformation content, and playful self-introduction trends that invite audience connection.
TK-01
"Loving Life Again" Glow-Up Format
Post-reset content showing small wins and emotional shifts back to balance drives saves and shares.
Ella Langley's track powers a flexible format pairing aesthetic b-roll with text overlays about cutting negativity or returning to form. The format works across wellness, lifestyle, and service brands because it taps into universal emotional relief—progress without pressure.
DOING IT WELL
Wellness & productivity creators
Using the sound to frame "back on track" routines with calm morning montages and consistency wins
Dating & finance app brands
Showing everyday moments paired with text like "loving life again because I feel in control"

"Self Aware" Cinematic Carousel Trend
Moody visuals with thoughtful text overlays build emotional depth in under 15 seconds.
Temper City's audio (434K+ videos) anchors dreamy scenery carousels and hot-take overlays. The format is flexible—calm, confident, or quietly empowering—and thrives when paired with aesthetic visuals that feel personal and memorable. It's algorithm-friendly because completion rate stays high.
DOING IT WELL
Travel & lifestyle creators
Pairing scenic footage with introspective captions about growth or mindset shifts
Brands with strong visual identity
Using product in context with reflective text that feels intentional, not promotional

"If You Wanna Get With Me" Self-Intro Format
Post-viral follower surges need context—this format reintroduces your vibe in one clip.
The Altégo remix of "Bass Down Low" powers a lipsync format where creators explain their niche, content style, or brand values via text overlays. It's perfect for accounts riding algorithm momentum who need to orient new followers quickly. Comedy and self-awareness win engagement.

Instagram
Transformation Reels, editorial energy, and carousel storytelling
Instagram's April momentum sits at the intersection of before/after formats, Tame Impala cinematic vibes, and nostalgic carousel templates that drive high save rates.
IG-01
"World Stop!" Transformation Trend
One-take before-and-after Reels dominate beauty, fashion, and home content with high watch-through rates.
The @browsbyzulema audio format pauses mid-flow, completes a transformation (makeup, outfit, room refresh), then resumes with "carry on." It's simple, dramatic, and satisfying—keeping viewers engaged through completion. The format works because the payoff is visual and instant.
DOING IT WELL
Beauty and brow accounts
Showing dramatic makeup or brow transformations with the pause-and-reveal mechanic
Fashion & fitness creators
Using outfit changes or workout progressions to showcase results in under 20 seconds
HIGH RETENTION

IG-02
Tame Impala Cinematic Content Wave
Moody, editorial-style Reels with slow movement and dark palettes are outperforming high-energy formats.
Tame Impala tracks (503K+ videos for one track alone) anchor aesthetic-forward content: portfolio teasers, BTS process footage, and luxury product showcases. Creators are using the cinematic energy for feel-over-information content that prioritizes mood and visual standard.
DOING IT WELL
Photographers & creatives
Sharing portfolio work with close-up textures and no voiceover—let the work speak
Real estate & luxury brands
Walking through properties or products with cinematic pacing and muted color grading

IG-03
Mindset Shift Carousel Format
Split-screen carousels contrasting childhood vs. adult perspectives are driving exceptional save rates.
Carousels comparing simplicity of childhood thinking with complexity of adult life resonate because they're universally relatable. The format uses bold text on minimal backgrounds and performs best when the contrast is sharp and emotionally honest. Saves indicate long-term value.
DOING IT WELL
Lifestyle & mental health creators
Using clean typography to contrast past/present mindsets with emotional honesty
Personal development brands
Creating shareable carousel templates that audiences screenshot and revisit

LinkedIn
Native documents, dwell time strategy, and carousel dominance
LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm rewards depth, not frequency. Native documents score 7% engagement, carousels earn 17x more interactions than images, and clicks now matter more than vanity metrics.
LI-01
Native Document Dominance
PDF carousels and native docs are the #1 performing format, with 7% engagement and 14% YoY growth.
LinkedIn's algorithm prioritizes content that keeps users on-platform. Native documents (carousels, PDFs) outperform video and single images because they increase dwell time and encourage swipes. The format signals thought leadership and works best when sharing frameworks, case studies, or timely commentary.
DOING IT WELL
B2B thought leaders
Repurposing blog content into 6-10 slide carousels with one key takeaway per slide
Marketing agencies
Sharing campaign results or industry data in carousel format to drive saves and shares
ALGORITHM BOOST
HIGH DWELL
SAVES
LI-02
Clicks Are the New Currency
Engagement rates jumped from 12.21% to 13.90% in 2026, driven by invisible interactions like clicks and swipes.
LinkedIn tracks every carousel swipe and "see more" click. These actions factor into engagement rate even though they're invisible. Quality and clickability now matter more than post frequency. Content that encourages interaction—carousels, polls, external links—earns more algorithmic reach.
DOING IT WELL
Data-driven content creators
Front-loading insights in the first 2 lines to earn the "see more" tap, then delivering value
Consultants & coaches
Using posts with external links strategically—2-5 high-quality posts/week outperform daily generic posting

LI-03
Raw, Behind-the-Scenes Video
Unpolished 3-6 minute horizontal clips outlining thought processes are outperforming highly produced video.
LinkedIn audiences crave substance over production value. Raw BTS videos showing frameworks, decision-making, or project breakdowns perform because they feel helpful and non-promotional. Video views dropped 36% overall, but authentic, horizontal-format clips explaining real work still drive engagement.
DOING IT WELL
Founders & operators
Self-recording project debriefs or decision frameworks in one take with no editing
Marketing teams
Repurposing live session clips or team process videos to show real work, not polished ads

YouTube
Episodic formats, Shorts monetization, and TV-first thinking
YouTube's 2026 landscape rewards serialized storytelling, authentic unscripted content, and dual-format strategies that serve both mobile Shorts and living-room long-form viewers.
YT-01
Serialized, TV-Style Content
Creators are embracing recurring formats and multi-part series to build habit-driven viewership.
Nostalgic TV-style formats are back: episodic series, recurring characters, and structured story arcs keep viewers returning. Creators like Druski launched limited series (Coulda Been House, Coulda Been Love) with binge appeal but no fatigue. The format works because it creates expectation and completion behavior.
DOING IT WELL
Druski
Big Brother-style reality series with creator chaos, under 10 episodes for digestibility
Animation studios (Glitch)
Launching "Glitch Direct" BTS format to pull fans into the production pipeline transparently
HABIT BUILDING

RECURRING
YT-02
Authentic, Unscripted Content Surge
Over-produced videos underperform as audiences favor raw, unfiltered storytelling that builds trust.
Viewers are gravitating toward content that feels human: simpler thumbnails, relaxed pacing, and meandering storylines. Creators like Sam Sulek and Dry Creek Wrangler School are posting uncut videos with sincere, life-lesson energy. The shift prioritizes intimacy over spectacle, and authenticity over polish.
DOING IT WELL
Sam Sulek
Uncut gym footage with authentic voiceover, no fancy edits, creating parasocial intimacy
Environmental & mission-driven orgs
Empowering team to self-record field updates with genuine storytelling, not scripted messaging

YT-03
Shorts as Discovery, Long-Form as Depth
YouTube Shorts (90B daily views) act as funnels to long-form content and channel subscriptions.
Shorts monetization ($0.03-$0.10 per 1K views) is lower than long-form, but the real value is discovery. Creators use Shorts for 15-45 second hooks, then guide viewers to 8-20 minute structured videos. The dual-format strategy serves mobile discovery and TV watch sessions simultaneously.
DOING IT WELL
Multi-format creators
Producing vertical Shorts for discovery, repurposing long-form into cutdowns with clear CTAs
Educational channels
Using Shorts to tease concepts, linking to full tutorials or deep-dive explainers for completion
