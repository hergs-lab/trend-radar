TikTok

TK-01
Glitch-Edit Stuck Frames
Charli XCX's "Rock Music" powers intermediate edits using Instagram's stuck-frame animation timed to the glitch moment.
The format uses fast edits with frozen frames synced to audio drops, creating a high-energy visual that stops the scroll. Works particularly well for product reveals, fit checks, and transformation content where the "stuck" moment builds anticipation.
Doing It Well
Alo Yoga
Uses minimalist studio shots with glitch transitions to showcase serene stretches and product drops.
Small creators (under 50K)
Reporting millions of views on first attempts with this audio-driven format.
Algorithm boost Audio-driven Intermediate edit
TK-02
Companion-Style Videos
Viewers want company through screens — "study with me," "work a 9-5 with me," and "get ready with me" formats pull huge engagement.
The trend reflects a shift toward parasocial companionship content that feels like hanging out with a friend. Lo-fi, unpolished, one-take videos outperform studio-grade productions because they feel native to the platform and genuinely relatable. TikTok rewards the authenticity signal.
Doing It Well
Chamberlain Coffee
Handheld one-take videos that feel like casual check-ins from a friend — never overproduced.
Lifestyle micro-creators
Building loyal audiences with daily rituals and unfiltered moments that feel like co-presence.
Parasocial Lo-fi Relatable
TK-03
The "Wow, Ok" Acting Range Challenge
Breakout no-audio performance format where creators show off emotional range in seven-second lip-sync challenges.
The format rewards personality-led performance without requiring specific trending audio, making it accessible to all account types including business accounts. It's part of a broader shift toward skill-based content that lets creators demonstrate range, humor, or storytelling chops in a highly shareable format.
Doing It Well
Performance-driven creators
Using exaggerated facial expressions and quick cuts to showcase comedic timing and character work.
Brand accounts (business-safe)
No specific audio required makes this format accessible to corporate profiles testing personality content.
No audio needed Performance Business-safe

Instagram

IG-01
"Seeing if the Algorithm Prefers"
Pit a high-effort edit against a deadpan static shot in the same Reel and let the algorithm — and audience — decide.
This self-aware meta format has been climbing for two weeks straight. It's format-driven with no specific audio required, making it fully accessible to business accounts. The trend taps into creator and audience curiosity about what the algorithm actually rewards, turning A/B testing into entertainment.
Doing It Well
Content strategists
Using side-by-side comparisons to test polished vs raw content while engaging audiences in the result.
Brands with in-house teams
Turning internal debates about production quality into relatable, transparent content experiments.
Meta-commentary Business-safe Format-driven
IG-02
Flash Filter Y2K Transformations
Instagram's new AI Flash filter turns regular photos into harsh-flash late-2000s digicam shots — creators are racing with before-and-afters.
The nostalgia-driven filter taps into Y2K aesthetic revival and the cultural fascination with lo-fi, unpolished photography. It's driving massive cross-platform engagement as creators export the effect to TikTok. The trend works because it combines easy-to-use tooling with emotional triggers around early internet aesthetics.
Doing It Well
Fashion micro-influencers
Using the filter to recreate 2000s paparazzi shots and vintage party vibes with modern outfits.
Gen Z lifestyle creators
Leaning into nostalgia for an era they barely remember — creating aspirational "early internet" aesthetics.
Nostalgia Y2K aesthetic Filter-driven
IG-03
Trending Audio on Carousels
You can now add trending audio to carousels and single photos — opening them up to Reels feed distribution and Explore placement.
This platform update fundamentally changes content strategy. Previously static formats can now ride trending sounds for algorithmic lift. Early adopters are seeing carousels appear in Reels feeds alongside video content, dramatically expanding reach for educational, product, and storytelling posts that work better as swipeable formats.
Doing It Well
Educational creators
Pairing infographic carousels with trending audio to get Explore page visibility previously reserved for Reels.
E-commerce brands
Adding viral sounds to product carousels to boost discovery and drive traffic from Reels feed placement.
Platform update Reach expansion Carousel strategy

LinkedIn

LI-01
Native Carousels Dominate Engagement
Native document posts (carousels/PDFs) lead all formats with a 7% engagement rate — a 14% increase year-over-year.
Carousels continue earning the highest reach (1,451 impressions per post on average) because they encourage swipes and extended dwell time. LinkedIn's algorithm now tracks every "see more" click and carousel swipe as an engagement signal. The format supports both educational content and storytelling, making it effective across funnel stages.
Doing It Well
B2B marketers and consultants
Using carousels for frameworks, case studies, and thought leadership that rewards deeper engagement.
SaaS founders and product teams
Sharing product updates and feature walkthroughs in swipeable formats that keep users on-platform.
Highest engagement Algorithm reward Educational
LI-02
Raw Behind-the-Scenes Process Content
Audiences crave substance and depth — raw, unpolished 3-6 minute clips outlining frameworks and thought processes are driving engagement.
LinkedIn users are starved for simple, helpful information delivered without over-polished "thought leader" performance. Horizontal-style clips showing real work, decision-making frameworks, and unfiltered commentary are outperforming scripted corporate content. The algorithm rewards authenticity signals and sustained watch time.
Doing It Well
Agency founders and operators
Sharing screen recordings and workflow walkthroughs that show how decisions get made in real time.
Marketing strategists
Posting lo-fi video breakdowns of campaign strategies without scripts or heavy editing — just expertise.
Authenticity Watch time Expertise-led
LI-03
Invisible Interactions (Clicks Over Likes)
Clicks are doing the heavy lifting — average clicks per post rose 4.90% while visible metrics like likes stayed flat.
LinkedIn's algorithm now prioritizes "invisible" engagement signals like carousel swipes, link clicks, and "see more" expansions over public reactions. This shift rewards content that drives deeper interaction and dwell time rather than performative engagement. Quality and clickability matter more than posting frequency.
Doing It Well
Content strategists using analytics tools
Tracking click-through behavior with tools like Taplio and Shield to optimize for actual engagement depth.
Personal brand builders
Creating curiosity-driven hooks and external links that generate clicks rather than passive scrolling.
Algorithm shift Hidden metrics Depth over volume

YouTube

YT-01
Recurring Franchise Formats
Viewers crave structure and serialized content — recurring formats like "feeding starving celebrities" or reality franchises are driving massive growth.
Creators like Quenlin Blackwell and Druski built multi-episode franchises that turn viewers into loyal audiences who need to watch everything to "complete" the narrative. The trend reflects YouTube's shift toward appointment viewing and binge-worthy content structures rather than one-off viral hits.
Doing It Well
Quenlin Blackwell
Humor-driven interview series "feeding starving celebrities" that builds anticipation for each new guest.
Druski
Reality franchise "Coulda Been Records/House/Love" attracted 710K viewers through serialized storytelling.
Serialized Appointment viewing Franchise model
YT-02
Shorts-First Storytelling
Shorts solve three problems at once — they lower production friction, test messaging fast, and match mobile attention patterns.
YouTube Shorts dominate because they provide emotional clarity within seconds and serve as a testing ground for longer content. Brands are using Shorts to validate messaging, build familiarity, and drive traffic to long-form videos. The format rewards speed, authenticity, and commercial intent packaged for easy consumption.
Doing It Well
Tutorial and how-to creators
Using 30-second Shorts as teasers for full tutorials while building audience familiarity with their teaching style.
Small businesses
Testing product demos and messaging in Shorts before investing in polished long-form video production.
Mobile-first Testing ground Low friction
YT-03
Long-Form TV Viewing Experience
YouTube's algorithm now prioritizes viewer satisfaction over follower count — longer, immersive formats thrive, especially on TV screens.
The rise of "TV watchers" marks a shift from passive scrolling to intentional sit-down viewing. Longer story-led videos that hold attention and deepen engagement are being rewarded. The algorithm focuses on session time and completion rates, making content series and multi-part narratives more valuable than standalone clips.
Doing It Well
Glitch Studio
Behind-the-scenes "Glitch Direct" format previews upcoming animation series in casual, binge-worthy vlog style.
Documentary-style creators
Building 20-40 minute deep dives that function as appointment viewing on connected TVs and larger screens.
TV screens Watch time Immersive
