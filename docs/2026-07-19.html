TikTok

Chaotic Inner Monologue & Before/After Transitions

TK-01

Chaotic Inner Monologue

Painfully relatable videos showing overstimulated brains trying to think about 37 things at once.

Creators pace aimlessly around the house or stand frozen in the kitchen while chaotic inner monologue audio plays. The format captures ADHD-style overthinking in a way that resonates deeply with viewers experiencing decision paralysis or executive dysfunction.

DOING IT WELL
Product/App Creators
Perfect for showing the "before state" your product solves
Wellness & Productivity Accounts
Authentic representation of mental load builds trust
RELATABLE AUDIO-DRIVEN LOW PRODUCTION
TK-02

Out With the Old Transitions

Before/after transformations set to beat-drop audio — showing the upgrade from old routine to new solution.

Creators show the "before" (old habit, messy setup, frustrating situation), then as the beat changes, transition into the new version with a clear visual upgrade. Works brilliantly for product reveals, lifestyle improvements, and service demonstrations.

DOING IT WELL
@applewithsarah
Clean iPhone upgrade transitions with satisfying visual payoff
Home & Organization Creators
Room makeovers and decluttering transformations drive saves
HIGH RETENTION SATISFYING PRODUCT-FRIENDLY
TK-03

Saxophones Getting Louder

Ordinary situations made dramatic with "something's about to go terribly wrong" audio overlay.

Using the dramatic saxophone audio, creators turn completely normal moments into suspenseful narratives with text overlay explaining why they're slowly realizing they're in trouble. The absurd contrast between mundane reality and escalating audio creates comedic tension.

DOING IT WELL
@parkerpannell
Situational comedy that pairs everyday mishaps with trending sound
Financial Apps & Productivity Tools
"Choosing the hard way instead of the app" creates relatable problem-solution hook

Instagram

Sketch-to-Reality & Camera Wipe Reveals

IG-01

Sketch-to-Outfit Animation

Hand-drawn animation overlay where each clothing item is sketched onto the creator then transforms into the real piece.

One of the most visually satisfying formats on Reels right now. Each piece of the outfit is illustrated directly onto the creator's body one item at a time, and as the sketch completes, it seamlessly transforms into actual clothing. The format pairs with orchestral tracks for elevated aesthetic appeal.

DOING IT WELL
@taliadunlop
Popularized the format with clean execution and smooth transitions
Fashion & Retail Brands
Product showcase that feels artistic rather than promotional
HIGH SAVES CREATIVE GRWM EVOLUTION
IG-02

Wait, Let Me Wipe the Camera

Cleanest product and location reveal format — empty space, hand swipe across lens, then finished transformation.

Originated by @james.and.antler, a Brisbane coffee bar. The format shows an underwhelming or empty space, a hand swiping across the camera, then reveals the finished look or product placement. Venues and product brands are seeing strong performance because the transition feels native to social rather than ad-like.

DOING IT WELL
@james.and.antler
Created original audio now powering thousands of venue reveals
Hospitality & Retail Spaces
Before/after format perfect for showcasing fit-outs and spaces
TRANSITION VENUE-FRIENDLY ORIGINAL AUDIO
IG-03

Netflix Documentary Confessional

Turning mundane moments into full dramatic documentary-style confessionals — unwarranted drama is the point.

Creators sit down, look into camera, take a breath, and treat the most ordinary moments (morning routine, Tuesday, your job) like a high-stakes Netflix true crime interview. The absurd contrast between trivial subject matter and serious delivery creates comedic effect and high engagement.

DOING IT WELL
Lifestyle & Personal Brand Creators
Self-aware humor that humanizes the creator builds connection
B2C Brands with Personality
Mockumentary style makes brand storytelling feel entertaining
COMEDY STORYTELLING TALKING HEAD
LinkedIn

Document Carousels & Long-Form Expertise

LI-01

Native Document Posts (PDFs)

Carousels crushing all other formats with 6.6-7% engagement rate — highest on the platform.

Native document posts (PDF carousels) are hitting unprecedented engagement rates because they align with how users consume content on LinkedIn: swipeable, educational, and save-worthy. They generate 278% higher engagement than video and 17x more interactions than single images, yet are still underused relative to their performance.

DOING IT WELL
B2B Thought Leaders
Frameworks, case studies, and data breakdowns in multi-slide format
Professional Services Firms
Educational content that demonstrates expertise without sales pitch
HIGHEST ENGAGEMENT EDUCATIONAL SAVE-WORTHY
LI-02

500-800 Word Story Posts

Long-form posts with personal story + specific insight outperforming short posts — the hook determines click-through.

LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm rewards longer, substantive posts that demonstrate genuine expertise. Posts of 500-800 words with a personal story, specific insight, or detailed analysis consistently generate more reach. The first two lines matter enormously because LinkedIn truncates with "see more" — strong hooks determine whether readers expand the post.

DOING IT WELL
Founders & CEOs
Executive-authored content generates 4x engagement of average posts
Industry Commentators
Hot takes and analysis on news/trends spark debate in comments
THOUGHT LEADERSHIP DWELL TIME HOOK-DRIVEN
LI-03

Employee & Founder Voice

Personal profiles outperform company pages by 2.75x — employee advocacy is your highest-leverage distribution channel.

Employee-authored posts generate roughly double the engagement of identical content from company pages. Employee reshares extend company page reach by 561% because content distributes through personal networks. Most organizations still aren't structuring content to make employee sharing easy, creating opportunity for teams that do.

DOING IT WELL
Tech Companies with Advocacy Programs
Coordinated employee sharing amplifies brand reach authentically
Leadership Teams
CEO content performs 4x better than brand handle alone
AMPLIFICATION AUTHENTICITY NETWORK EFFECT
YouTube

Serialized TV Formats & Shorts Discovery

YT-01

TV-Style Serialized Content

Creators embracing traditional TV formats with recurring episodes, structured arcs, and binge-worthy storytelling.

YouTube creators are building serialized videos that feel like TV shows — recurring formats, character development, and episodic structure. Viewers prefer channels offering structured arcs and deeper value over one-off videos. Examples include reality-style series, behind-the-scenes formats previewing upcoming content, and limited-run shows under 10 episodes.

DOING IT WELL
Druski (Coulda Been House)
Big Brother-style reality format reimagined for YouTube with creator chaos
Glitch Studio (Glitch Direct)
Behind-the-scenes vlog-style format previewing animated series builds anticipation
BINGE-WORTHY RECURRING LONG-FORM
YT-02

Shorts as Discovery Engine

YouTube Shorts hitting 200B daily views — now monetizable and feeding long-form subscriptions.

Shorts crossed 70 billion daily views and creators can now earn money from them ($0.03-$0.10 per 1,000 views). The real value isn't per-view rate — it's the funnel. Shorts act as discovery mechanism feeding long-form content and channel subscriptions. Top formats: mini vlogs, transformation videos, storytime micro-dramas, and quick tutorials.

DOING IT WELL
Educational Creators
Algorithm distributes educational Shorts from small accounts aggressively
Multi-Format Channels
Using Shorts for discovery + long-form for retention outperforms Shorts-only
SHORT-FORM DISCOVERY MONETIZATION
YT-03

Authenticity Over Polish

Creators ditching highly-produced videos for authentic, unfiltered content — intimacy beats production value.

Thumbnails are getting simpler, fancy edits are disappearing, and storylines have relaxed pacing. Creators are shifting toward sincere videos that create intimacy with viewers. Raw, unpolished behind-the-scenes content and uncut life lessons are outperforming overproduced formats because audiences crave genuine connection.

Dry Creek Wrangler School

RAW INTIMACY ANTI-POLISH
