TikTok

TK-01

Glitch-Edit Audio Trend

Charli XCX's "Rock Music" drives stuck-frame reveals synced to the song's deliberate glitch moment.
Creators freeze a highlight frame mid-motion while audio continues, creating a satisfying pause-and-payoff mechanic. Works for product reveals, outfit transitions, and any visual moment worth lingering on. The format is intermediate-level editing but rewards high completion rates.
Doing it well
Product & fashion creators
Using Instagram's stuck-frame animation to freeze reveals at the glitch beat drop
Food content channels
Holding on final plated shot or iced coffee pour for satisfying visual payoff
AUDIO-DRIVEN EDIT TREND RISING
TK-02

2026 Summer Anthem

Josh Fawaz's "Like a Prayer" remix delivers instant nostalgia in seven-second lip-sync clips with zero storyline required.
Small creators report millions of views on first attempts because the format removes every friction point. No B-roll, no concept, just camera + lip-sync + on-screen text reading "2026 Summer Anthem". The Madonna sample does all the cultural heavy lifting, creating free emotional pull.
Doing it well
Emerging creators under 50K
Achieving viral reach with minimal production through sheer format accessibility
Brand accounts using founder face
Humanizing product moments with team member cameos or product-personified angles
LOW-LIFT VIRAL ENTRY POINT NOSTALGIA
TK-03

Descriptive Hashtags Return

Specific hashtags now drive 114% more traffic than last year as the algorithm uses them for video placement.
The shift from generic #fyp to niche descriptors like #pastarecipes or #weeknightdinner helps TikTok categorize content for interest-graph distribution. Using 1-5 targeted hashtags outperforms broad reach plays, allowing smaller accounts to surface in relevant searches and discovery feeds.
Doing it well
Niche tutorial creators
Pairing searchable captions with specific hashtags to rank in TikTok and Google search
Small business accounts
Using category-specific tags to reach high-intent audiences without follower count
ALGORITHM SHIFT SEARCH-LED DISCOVERY NICHE TARGETING

Instagram

IG-01

YAP Format (Unpolished Selfie Reels)

Minimally edited, off-the-cuff selfie videos with subtitles and text hooks counter algorithm fatigue and AI oversaturation.
The format performs because it feels immediately human in a feed saturated with over-edited and AI-generated content. Creators film single-take talking-head clips with simple text overlays, delivering value without production gloss. Some YAP Reels from personal brand creators reach 800K+ views by prioritizing clarity and authenticity over perfection.
Doing it well
Max Tornow
Entrepreneur using raw selfie format to share business insights, reaching 800K+ views per Reel
Personal brand coaches
Leveraging unscripted energy to build trust and counter over-polished brand content
AUTHENTICITY ANTI-POLISH LOW-PRODUCTION
IG-02

DM Shares Drive Algorithm

Direct message shares now carry 3-5x more algorithmic weight than likes, rewarding content people trust enough to send to friends.
Instagram's 2026 algorithm interprets DM shares as a trust signal, expanding reach far beyond passive engagement. Content that sparks private conversation gets broader distribution. This shift favors shareable formats like relatable carousels, transformation posts, and educational saves over vanity metrics.
Doing it well
Educational carousel creators
Designing swipeable posts that friends send as references or recommendations
Micro-community builders
Using keyword triggers and DM automation to convert shares into direct engagement
ALGORITHM UPDATE SHAREABILITY TRUST METRIC
IG-03

Trial Reels Testing

Instagram's Trial Reels feature lets creators A/B test content with non-followers before publishing to their grid.
This experimental tool helps creators test hooks, formats, and content angles by distributing a Reel to new audiences first. Performance data from the trial run informs whether to publish the final version. It reduces risk for content experiments and gives insight into what resonates beyond existing followers.
Doing it well
Growth-focused creators
Testing new formats and hooks with non-followers to validate before main grid publishing
Brand content teams
Using Trial Reels to reduce creative risk and validate messaging before full campaign rollout
PLATFORM FEATURE A/B TESTING RISK REDUCTION

LinkedIn

LI-01

Carousel PDFs Dominate Engagement

Multi-page PDF carousels deliver 7% engagement rates and 39% more reach than standard posts—LinkedIn's algorithmic gold.
Carousels increase dwell time as users swipe through pages, which the algorithm rewards with extended visibility. The format works for repurposing blog posts, presenting data-driven insights, and building swipe-through storytelling. Native documents outperform single images and video for reach and saves.
Doing it well
B2B thought leaders
Turning case studies and frameworks into visual carousels that earn saves and profile visits
Agency founders
Repurposing reports and insights into branded carousel templates for recurring content
NATIVE FORMAT HIGHEST ENGAGEMENT DWELL TIME
LI-02

Saves > Likes for Reach

Saves are now valued 5x higher than likes in LinkedIn's algorithm, signaling content users want to reference later.
The 2026 algorithm shift prioritizes depth and usefulness over passive scrolling. Posts that get saved become long-term assets in users' collections and surface in feeds over weeks, not hours. Educational content, frameworks, and how-to posts perform best under this shift.
Doing it well
Subject matter experts
Creating reference-quality posts users save as resources for ongoing work
SaaS and service brands
Posting frameworks and templates designed for bookmark-and-use behavior
ALGORITHM SHIFT UTILITY FOCUS LONG-TAIL REACH
LI-03

Personal Profiles Outperform Pages

Personal profiles earn 5x more engagement than company pages as LinkedIn rewards authentic human-to-human connection.
Company page organic reach has dropped to 1-2% of followers, while personal profiles maintain 500% higher distribution. Brands shift to employee advocacy and founder-led content to break through. The algorithm evaluates expertise and rewards posts from credentialed individuals over faceless brand accounts.
Doing it well
Founder-led brands
Posting from CEO/founder profiles with personal insights rather than company announcements
Employee advocacy programs
Enabling subject matter experts to share company stories from personal accounts
DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY HUMAN-LED FOUNDER VOICE

YouTube

YT-01

Shorts + Long-Form Hybrid Strategy

Channels mixing Shorts for discovery with long-form for retention and monetization outperform single-format strategies.
Shorts drive 200 billion daily views and act as top-of-funnel discovery, while 10+ minute videos build watch time and ad revenue. Creators use Shorts to test hooks and ideas, then expand winning concepts into long-form deep dives. The strongest 2026 channels treat both formats as complementary, not competing.
Doing it well
Educational creators
Using Shorts to surface quick tips, then funneling viewers to detailed tutorials
Entertainment channels
Testing viral concepts in Shorts before investing in high-production long-form content
DUAL-FORMAT DISCOVERY + DEPTH MONETIZATION
YT-02

TV-Style Serialized Content

Recurring formats and episodic series build binge appeal and viewer anticipation, rewarded by YouTube's satisfaction-based algorithm.
Creators embrace structured storytelling with recurring characters, challenges, and season-like arcs. Formats like reality franchises, interview series, and recurring challenges keep audiences returning. The shift mirrors traditional TV but with creator authenticity, offering familiarity and momentum that one-off videos can't match.
Doing it well
Druski (Coulda Been franchise)
Building reality series with recurring formats that attracted 710K viewers in a year
Glitch Studio
Launching behind-the-scenes vlog series that pull fans into production pipeline
EPISODIC RECURRING FORMAT BINGE APPEAL
YT-03

Authenticity Over Polish

Unfiltered, minimally edited content drives greater trust and engagement as over-produced videos underperform in 2026.
Creators ditch high production for raw, personality-led formats that feel conversational. Simplified thumbnails, uncut talking-head videos, and meandering storytelling create intimacy over spectacle. Audiences respond to sincere, human content that counters AI-generated polish and algorithmic perfection.
Doing it well
Sam Sulek (bodybuilding)
Building massive following with uncut gym vlogs and raw, unscripted commentary
Dry Creek Wrangler School
Sharing uncut life lessons and horse training without heavy editing or production
RAW CONTENT ANTI-PRODUCTION TRUST-BUILDING
