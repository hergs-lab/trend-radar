TikTok
TK-01
"How My X Would Respond to a Bully"
Unscripted chaos with real stakes—format works on anyone from siblings to coworkers.
On-screen text sets up the scenario, then creators film genuine reactions to insults. Some deliver cold comebacks, others fumble spectacularly. The unpredictable nature drives comment engagement and duet potential.
DOING IT WELL
Family & Sibling Creators
Natural tension and authentic reactions make the format instantly relatable.
Workplace Comedy Accounts
Boss and coworker variations tap into universal workplace dynamics.

TK-02
Compressed Storytelling
Opening text explains the situation immediately, video supplies proof or punchline in seconds.
Winning posts don't promise—they deliver. The week of Aug 15–22 showed instant clarity beats mystery. Text overlay states the premise, visuals provide payoff. Works across comedy, transformation reveals, and candid story setups.
DOING IT WELL
RushTok & College Move-In Creators
Seasonal context paired with fast emotional payoffs drives high retention.
Transformation & Before/After Accounts

Short How-To as Search Engine Content
TikTok increasingly functions as a search engine—tight explainers earn bookmarks and repeat views.

"Worth the Money" List Creators

Instagram
IG-01
Bedazzling Revival
Loud, tactile transformation content—the opposite of quiet luxury and genuinely fun to watch assemble.
Started with jean jackets a decade ago, now evolved into hair tutorials with hot-fix gems, rhinestone-crusted skincare bottles, and crystal tech accessories. The process itself is the content—15–20 second Reels showing hands placing gems in real time outperform static reveals.
DOING IT WELL
Beauty & Hair Tutorial Creators
"Making hairstyles FUN again" clips turn basic updos into collectible moments.
DIY & Craft Accounts
Real-time transformation of plain objects satisfies process-watching urge.

"And for the Lady..." Trend
Noah Kahan's "End of August" soundtracks product reveals tailored for specific person types.
Text overlay reveals "the perfect product, place, or experience for a particular type of person." Easy brand format to showcase exactly who an offering is made for. Works across lifestyle, productivity, dating, and service categories.

IG-03
Context-First Reels
Format fragmentation favors hyper-specific communities over universal viral sounds.
Instead of one dance sweeping every category, winners are built around niche communities: college anxiety, gaming news, online friendships, parenting nostalgia, fandom edits, street fashion. Micro-creators with under 500 followers routinely hit millions of views by nailing content-market fit.
DOING IT WELL
Micro-Creators (Under 500 Followers)
Small accounts break through with tight audience alignment, not follower count.
Niche Community Accounts
Relatable micro-skits and serialized formats outperform generic trending audio.

LinkedIn
LI-01
Document Posts Dominating Engagement
Native documents (carousels/PDFs) hit 6.6–7% engagement rate, more than double video or single images.
LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm shift rewards clicks and swipes as engagement signals. Carousels earn 1,451 impressions per post on average. Format aligns with how users consume professional content—supports both education and storytelling across funnel stages.
DOING IT WELL
B2B Thought Leaders & Consultants
Frameworks and industry breakdowns in carousel format drive saves and shares.
Founders & Operators
Case studies and project outcomes in multi-slide format build trust.

LI-02
Raw, Unpolished Behind-the-Scenes
Authenticity beats polish—human-made, experience-based content outperforms sterile AI output.
3–6 minute horizontal clips outlining thought processes or frameworks perform exceptionally. People are starved for simple, helpful information. Over 40% of long-form LinkedIn posts now look AI-made, making authentic founder stories and employee posts more valuable.
DOING IT WELL
Employee Advocates & Team Members
Closer to the work, language contains detail, perspective feels less filtered.
Founder-Led Content
Personal lessons, failures, and trade-offs build trust faster than brand polish.

LI-03
Invisible Interaction Signals
Clicks are doing the heavy lifting—swipes through carousels and "see more" links boost engagement rates.
Between 2025 and 2026, average clicks per post rose 4.90% while posting frequency dropped 10%. LinkedIn tracks every carousel swipe and link click, factoring them into engagement rate. Quality and clickability now matter more than volume.
DOING IT WELL
Data-Driven Marketers
Posts with external links and multi-image formats encourage measured interaction.
Poll & Collaborative Article Creators
Polls achieve 8.9% engagement; collaborative articles hit 12.3%.

YouTube
YT-01
Shorts Dominate Discovery, Long-Form Builds Trust
61% of organic views come from Shorts feed—use it for discovery, long-form for depth and conversion.
Shorts reached 200 billion daily views in 2025. The winning strategy combines formats: Shorts for instant clarity and visual hooks, long-form for bingeable systems with sequels and playlists that move viewers toward demos or signups.
DOING IT WELL
IShowSpeed
Speed Goes Pro series turned sports chaos into structured, serialized content.
Educational & SaaS Creators
Mix Shorts for reach with tutorial playlists for session depth.

YT-02
Faceless AI Channels Scaling Fast
Voiceover, screen recordings, stock footage, or AI-generated visuals—no on-camera presence required.
One of the four formats doing real growth in 2026. AI-generated lifestyle content (Pakistani village life, Ghibli-style cooking) achieves 469 views per subscriber with high volume publishing (5 uploads weekly). Universal visual language needs no translation.
