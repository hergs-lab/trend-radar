Platform 01
TikTok
TK-01
Tiny Text Reveal
Small, barely-visible text that zooms in to reveal the message rewards attention and creates curiosity-driven engagement.
Creators shrink text using platform tools, then film themselves approaching the camera as the text enlarges to readability. It's low-lift production with high rewatch value because viewers want to catch what they missed.
DOING IT WELL
Brand accounts (teasing offers) – Using it to reveal Black Friday discounts and product launches
Personal brands (hot takes) – Delivering industry opinions that reward close attention
INTERACTIVE LOW PRODUCTION REWATCH DRIVER
TK-02
Reverse Psychology Sell
Starting with "reasons why you don't need to..." then passionately listing reasons TO buy flips traditional sales messaging.
The format opens with text saying you don't need the product, then the creator appears to deliver a heartfelt (often sarcastic) case for it. Works because it acknowledges buyer skepticism while building desire through contrast.
DOING IT WELL
Beauty & skincare creators – Selling makeup restocks and lash care with playful contradiction
E-commerce brands – Driving cart conversions by acknowledging purchase hesitation
CONVERSION FOCUS HUMOR AUDIO TREND
TK-03
Behind-the-Scenes Raw
Unfiltered, low-polish content showing real process and daily work is outperforming highly produced brand videos in 2026.
TikTok's algorithm shift toward authenticity means coulisses content, founder-led videos, and "day in the life" clips are earning higher engagement than polished ads. Small businesses and personal brands benefit most because this levels the production playing field.
DOING IT WELL
Small business founders – Showing packing orders, prepping inventory, real studio moments
Creator economy accounts – Honest income breakdowns and workflow transparency
AUTHENTICITY ALGORITHM FRIENDLY FOUNDER-LED
Platform 02
Instagram
IG-01
Housewife Self-Made Anthem
Tate McRae's "Housewife" is being flipped into aspirational content showing the work that funds the dream life creators want.
The audio pairs with B-roll of work, lifestyle, or side hustles, with text like "I just wanna make a lot of money and..." followed by how they'd spend it. It's manifestation meets mood board, perfect for lifestyle and business creators showing both grind and goal.
DOING IT WELL
Lifestyle creators – Pairing morning routines with financial goals and travel wishlists
Business coaches – Showing client work that funds freedom-based lifestyles
TRENDING AUDIO ASPIRATIONAL DUO CHOREOGRAPHY
IG-02
War Is Over (Tiny Wins)
John Lennon's "Happy Xmas (War Is Over)" is being used to celebrate small victories with text finishing "war is over..."
Creators add text reading "war is over." then finish the sentence with something they survived, won, or finally achieved—temps dropped enough for sweaters, client signed, peppermint drinks returned. It taps into seasonal relief and relatable micro-celebrations that drive saves and shares.
DOING IT WELL
Seasonal lifestyle accounts – Marking fall milestones and cozy-season moments
Freelance & founder accounts – Celebrating client wins and project completions
LICENSED AUDIO RELATABLE HOLIDAY SEASON
IG-03
Carousel Saves Over Reels
Educational carousels are outperforming Reels on engagement and saves, delivering 3x more reach than single-image posts.
Instagram's algorithm rewards dwell time, and multi-slide carousels (especially educational, step-by-step, or opinion-led) keep users swiping. They're also saved more often than video, making them a high-leverage organic format for teams without video production capacity.
DOING IT WELL
Marketing educators – Posting framework breakdowns and strategy templates
Design & creative accounts – Sharing before-and-after transformations with process slides
HIGH SAVES DWELL TIME EDUCATIONAL
Platform 03
LinkedIn
LI-01
Document Carousels Dominate
PDF carousel posts are hitting 6.60% engagement rates—the highest of any LinkedIn format in 2026.
LinkedIn's algorithm prioritizes dwell time, and document posts force users to swipe through slides, signaling quality content. They outperform standard text and video by creating structured, save-worthy educational content that LinkedIn's professional audience actively seeks and shares.
DOING IT WELL
B2B marketers & consultants – Sharing frameworks, playbooks, and case study breakdowns
Startup founders – Posting data-driven insights and transparent business metrics
6.6% ENGAGEMENT DWELL TIME SAVE-WORTHY
LI-02
Employee-Generated Content
Posts from employee personal profiles get 2x more engagement than company pages and drive 30% of brand engagement.
LinkedIn's algorithm favors personal accounts over corporate pages, and audiences connect more with real voices than brand messaging. Employee advocacy programs that provide content ideas (not scripts) and recognize participation are scaling reach without ad spend.
DOING IT WELL
Tech companies – Engineers and product managers sharing build stories and learnings
Professional services firms – Partners posting client insights and industry commentary
2X ENGAGEMENT EMPLOYEE ADVOCACY ORGANIC REACH
LI-03
Comment-First Strategy
Comments carry 8x more algorithmic weight than likes, and commenting on high-visibility posts before sharing your own content boosts reach.
The algorithm tracks comment thread engagement and reply depth. Creators and brands are strategically commenting on trending posts in their niche to build visibility before publishing, then sparking discussion in their own posts by asking direct questions that invite detailed responses.
DOING IT WELL
Thought leaders & consultants – Engaging in comment threads before posting original takes
B2B SaaS founders – Asking questions that generate 77% more comments than average posts
8X WEIGHT VISIBILITY HACK DISCUSSION-DRIVEN
Platform 04
YouTube
YT-01
Non-Verbal POV Shorts
Wordless, visual-driven Shorts that rely on physical storytelling work across languages and drive global reach.
Creators like Daniel LaBelle demonstrate scenarios entirely through movement and expression—no dialogue, just relatable situations told visually. This format travels well internationally, requires no voiceover production, and leverages YouTube's Shorts algorithm which prioritizes retention over verbosity.
DOING IT WELL
Daniel LaBelle – Physical comedy POV scenarios with universal visual appeal
Lifestyle & DIY creators – Showing transformations and processes without narration
GLOBAL REACH SHORTS OPTIMIZED LOW PRODUCTION
YT-02
Podcast Clips as Shorts
Video podcast episodes are being clipped into 30-60 second Shorts that drive traffic back to full episodes and build returning audiences.
One long-form recording generates dozens of short clips optimized for discovery. Creators are pulling highlight moments, controversial takes, or useful tips and formatting them as Shorts with captions, expanding reach beyond their subscriber base and funneling viewers to the full show.
DOING IT WELL
Business & interview podcasts – Clipping standout quotes and tactical advice into discoverable Shorts
Niche expert shows – Using Shorts Series to organize connected episodes
REPURPOSING SHORTS SERIES FUNNEL STRATEGY
YT-03
Mystery Investigation Format
Mystery investigation content leads YouTube entertainment trends with 433M views in November, driven by narrative arc and repeatable structure.
Videos that follow a question, investigation, and resolution structure keep viewers watching through strong narrative pacing. The format rewards curiosity gaps and clear storytelling, making it ideal for educational explainers, true crime, urban exploration, or product testing that unfolds as discovery.
DOING IT WELL
True crime & unsolved channels – Structuring episodes around investigation and reveal
Product review & testing creators – Framing reviews as questions with investigative pacing
433M VIEWS NARRATIVE ARC HIGH RETENTION
