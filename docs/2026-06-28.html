TikTok
What's Moving on the FYP
TK-01
Behind-the-Scenes "Reali-Tea" Content
Raw, unpolished process beats curated perfection — the algorithm rewards honesty over aesthetics.
TikTok's 2026 trend report identifies "Reali-Tea" as the dominant shift: audiences want real people, real process, and unedited moments. UGC-style, lo-fi content consistently outperforms polished production by up to 55% ROI, and brands showing behind-the-scenes process are seeing higher engagement than fantasy feeds.
Doing it well
Glitch Studio
Casual vlog-style "Glitch Direct" series shows production pipeline, making fans feel included in creative process
Government/civic brands
Teams showing real work environments and process documentation resonate stronger than glossy campaigns
Authenticity Algorithm shift Low-fi wins
TK-02
Audio-Led Nostalgia Formats
June 2026 rewards content tied to cultural moments people already care about — from World Cup to Toy Story 5 carousels.
Josh Fawaz's "Like a Prayer" remix is the unofficial 2026 summer anthem driving seven-second lip-sync videos. Charli XCX's "Rock Music" glitch edits and the Toy Story 5 "There Was a Time" parent-child carousel are blowing up. Trends move fast this month — most have narrow lift windows tied to active cultural moments like the FIFA World Cup and major album drops.
Doing it well
@jennifermika_
Dance choreo for Ariana Grande's "hate that i made u love me" using adamusic_ remix became the format template
Family/nostalgia creators
Photo carousels paired with emotional audio (Toy Story, childhood moments) drive high save rates and shares
Summer 2026 Cultural moments Move fast
TK-03
Food Jutsu & Product Transitions
Anime-style transition trends make product and food content shareable — visual satisfaction drives saves and loops.
The Food Jutsu anime-transition trend is blowing up food and product content in June 2026. Creators film a product or dish, sync to a trending track, then apply visual effects (freeze frames, glitch cuts, transition animations) that make ordinary items feel cinematic. The format works because it's repeatable, visually satisfying, and platform-native — no heavy production required.
Doing it well
Food creators (category)
Pairing plating moments with beat-synced transitions and text overlays creates high rewatch value
Small product brands
Using trending transition formats to showcase product details without heavy ad spend or polished shoots
Visual format Product content Rewatch value
Instagram
Reels & Feed Formats Performing Now
IG-01
Photo Carousel "Day in the Life" Series
Breaking daily routines into swipeable photo carousels outperforms video vlogs — encourages completion and saves.
Instead of filming a vlog, creators break their day into a series of photos capturing different moments or activities. Each slide reveals a new part of the story, encouraging people to keep swiping. It feels personal, easy to consume, and more intentional than a traditional photo dump. Carousels hit 0.72% engagement rate in 2026, up 30.9% from 2025.
Doing it well
Lifestyle micro-creators
Using 8–10 slides with captions timed to each image, making stories feel episodic and binge-worthy
Service/agency accounts
Showcasing project process or team day through carousel format instead of text-heavy case studies
Carousel boost Mobile-first Saves
IG-02
YAP Format: Unedited Talking-Head Reels
Minimally edited, off-the-cuff selfie videos with subtitles counter AI and over-edited content — authenticity = trust.
YAP videos are selfie-style Reels, usually off the cuff, no script, very minimal editing, with subtitles and a text headline. They counter the over-edited feel and fake AI content by just being real. Creators using this format report 800K+ views on individual posts because it signals genuine human voice in a feed increasingly filled with templated hooks and AI-generated content.
Doing it well
Max Tornow
Entrepreneur using YAP format for business insights; some Reels amassed over 800K views with no polish
Personal brand coaches
Direct-to-camera storytelling with strong hooks performs better than highly produced explainer videos
Authenticity wins Anti-AI Algorithm-friendly
IG-03
DM Shares Drive Distribution
Instagram algorithm weights DM shares 3–5× higher than likes — shareable beats likeable in 2026.
Adam Mosseri confirmed that DM shares (sends) carry the most weight for reaching new audiences. 100 likes matter less than 10 DM shares. When someone sends your Reel to a friend, they're vouching for it — Instagram rewards that trust signal with broader distribution. Content optimized for shareability (relatable, useful, funny, save-worthy) now outperforms content designed purely for vanity engagement.
Doing it well
Infographic/data creators
Saveable charts and stat-driven Reels get high DM share rates because audiences send them to specific people
Meme/relatable content accounts
"Tag a friend" and "send this to someone who…" formats intentionally designed for DM sharing behavior
Algorithm shift Shareability Distribution key
LinkedIn
B2B Content Formats Earning Engagement
LI-01
Native Carousels/PDF Documents
Carousel posts lead all formats with 7% engagement rate — users stay on-platform, algorithm rewards native content.
Native documents (carousels and PDFs) deliver a 7% engagement rate in 2026, representing a 14% increase year-over-year. Multi-image posts boast 3.71% engagement, more than double that of standard video or single images. LinkedIn's algorithm promotes content that keeps users on-platform, and carousels support both educational content and storytelling across different funnel stages.
Doing it well
B2B SaaS brands
Sharing transparent metrics, case studies, and founder stories in 8–10 slide carousels drives 47% higher engagement
Thought leaders (category)
Frameworks, data visualizations, and how-to content formatted as PDFs get saved and shared by decision-makers
Top format 2026 Native wins Saves & shares
LI-02
First-Hour Conversational Engagement
LinkedIn's June 2026 update prioritizes posts generating meaningful discussion within first 30–60 minutes — quality replies matter.
LinkedIn's algorithm now uses conversational relevance scoring, promoting posts that generate meaningful, topical discussions early. The first 60 minutes are critical — 50% of total impressions happen within the first 48 hours, with a significant portion on day one. Timing and immediate engagement (especially thoughtful comments, not just emojis) now directly impact distribution more than follower count.
Doing it well
Founders sharing real insights
Posting timely commentary on industry news with clear opinions sparks debate and drives early comment threads
Employee advocacy programs
Coordinated (not spammy) early engagement from genuine team members boosts first-hour conversational signals
June update Golden hour Conversation > likes
LI-03
Short-Form Native Video (Under 90 Sec)
LinkedIn is pushing native video in the feed — videos under 90 seconds with captions perform best, testimonials especially effective.
Video content on LinkedIn generates 5× more engagement than static posts, but the platform rewards short, native uploads (not YouTube links). Videos under 90 seconds perform best and are flagged as top LinkedIn post trends for 2026. Video testimonials offer social proof in a highly engaging format. LinkedIn's Head of Premium Content recommends creators lean into video as a primary format.
Doing it well
Recruiters & HR professionals
60-second videos on "Top 3 Resume Mistakes" with clear CTAs drive profile visits and DM conversions
Service-based businesses
Client testimonial clips (30–60 sec) uploaded natively generate trust and higher engagement than text case studies
Native video Under 90 sec Social proof
YouTube
Long-Form + Shorts Strategy
YT-01
Return of Serialized Long-Form
Viewers prefer structured arcs and recurring formats over one-offs — serialized content builds loyal audiences and watch time.
YouTube's 2026 trend report notes that creators are embracing serialized, TV-style formats. Druski's "Coulda Been House" reality series, Quenlin Blackwell's "feeding starving celebrities" recurring format, and IShowSpeed's "Speed Goes Pro" structured show all drove massive subscriber growth. Viewers crave structure, interesting recurring formats, and deeper value — not just viral one-offs.
Doing it well
Druski
Led reality franchises ("Coulda Been House," "Coulda Been Love") that attracted 710K viewers in a year with recurring formats
Educational channels
Creating repeatable series formats (e.g., "Week in Review," case study breakdowns) builds familiarity and subscriber retention
Series formats Watch time Audience depth
YT-02
Shorts for Discovery, Long-Form for Monetization
Channels mixing Shorts (discovery) with long-form (retention, revenue) outperform Shorts-only strategies — dual format wins.
After years of Shorts dominance, watch time on long-form (10+ minutes) is growing again. Shorts drive discovery and reach, but long-form drives watch time, ad revenue, and audience depth. The strongest 2026 channels publish both. YouTube is becoming three platforms at once: a Shorts discovery engine, a long-form streaming hub, and a search/education resource.
Doing it well
Hybrid creators (category)
Using Shorts to tease topics, then directing viewers to long-form deep dives for full context and monetization
Educational/how-to channels
Quick Shorts for algorithm reach, comprehensive tutorials (10–20 min) for search traffic and AdSense revenue
Dual strategy Monetization Format balance
YT-03
Authenticity Over High Production
Creators ditching polished videos for authentic, unfiltered content — simplicity, intimacy, and sincerity resonate more in 2026.
Thumbnails are getting simpler, fancy edits are disappearing, and storylines have a relaxed, meandering pace focused on creating intimacy with viewers. Sam Sulek and Dry Creek Wrangler School exemplify this shift: uncut videos sharing life lessons and real process outperform heavily edited content. Audiences reward genuine expertise and human voice over production value.
Doing it well
Sam Sulek
Bodybuilder posting raw, unedited gym content with minimal thumbnails — grew rapidly by prioritizing intimacy over polish
Dry Creek Wrangler School
Pivoted to uncut videos sharing life lessons; simple thumbnails and authentic storytelling drive loyal following
Authenticity Low production Intimacy wins
